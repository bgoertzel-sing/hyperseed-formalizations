# The Leaky Transcension Hypothesis

**Source:** [bengoertzel.substack.com/p/the-leaky-transcension-hypothesis](https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/the-leaky-transcension-hypothesis)
**Date:** 2026-04-25

## Summary

Extends John Smart's Transcension Hypothesis with a 'leaky' variant: superintelligences don't fully withdraw into inner space but maintain partial interfaces with lower-complexity realms, creating a graduated ecology of intelligence rather than a sharp transcension boundary.

## Hyperseed Ontology Interpretation

the fiber bundle of intelligence levels has a leaky connection rather than a disconnected one; the d-calculus parallel transport across complexity levels preserves partial information, with curvature measuring the degree of interface maintenance
