# Claim inventory — God Doesn't Double-Count Evidence

Source: Ben Goertzel, "God Doesn't Double-Count Evidence,"
Eurykosmotron, 2022-01-15.

### Core claims from the article

1. **No double-counting principle.** Rational inference must not count the
   same evidence twice; doing so inflates confidence beyond what evidence
   warrants. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Physics-computation isomorphism.** The universe as computation enforces
   non-redundancy of evidence processing as a structural feature. Conservation
   laws are the universe's mechanism for not double-counting evidence within
   any local reference frame. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Information-theoretic consequence.** Double-counting inflates confidence
   beyond what evidence warrants — it produces systematically overconfident
   conclusions that a calibrated reasoner would reject. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Bayesian discipline.** Correct Bayesian updating naturally avoids
   double-counting, but practical systems (including human cognition) often
   fail at this because tracking evidence provenance is computationally
   expensive. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **AGI design requirement.** AGI reasoning engines must be designed with
   explicit mechanisms to prevent double-counting — this is not optional
   engineering but a fundamental correctness criterion. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Conservation = non-redundancy.** Physical conservation laws (energy,
   momentum, charge) are reinterpretable as evidence non-redundancy
   constraints. "God doesn't cook the books." *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **PLN relevance.** PLN's truth-value formulas handle evidence overlap
   via the confidence parameter, which tracks total evidence weight and
   prevents double-counting when evidence streams merge. *(inferred)*

### Hyperseed-ontology claims

8. **Fiber independence.** Independent evidence = independent fibers;
   double-counting = treating dependent fibers as independent. The fiber
   structure of the Hyperseed epistemic bundle makes evidence dependencies
   geometrically visible. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

9. **Homotopy independence certificate.** Homotopic evidence paths (shared
   derivation) must not be double-counted; only non-homotopic paths
   contribute independently. The homotopy class is the canonical
   independence certificate. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

10. **Base-space information budget.** Physical laws enforce non-redundancy
    as a base-space constraint: total evidence weight at each base point
    is bounded by an information capacity W_max(b). *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

11. **Divergence-free evidence current (d-calculus).** In the d-calculus
    formulation, evidence conservation becomes ∇ · J_evidence = 0 — the
    evidence current is divergence-free, ensuring no evidence is created
    or destroyed in transport. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

12. **Holonomy as double-counting detector.** Non-trivial holonomy in the
    evidence connection reveals "leaks" in evidential bookkeeping —
    transporting evidence around a closed loop and getting a different answer
    means the books are being cooked. The no-double-counting principle
    demands trivial holonomy within each reference frame. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

13. **Curvature = evidence interaction.** Non-zero curvature of the evidence
    connection indicates that evidence items are not independent. Flat
    connection = fully independent evidence = free fusion. Curved connection
    = interacting evidence = fusion requires holonomy correction.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

14. **Evidence-energy duality.** Evidence is to logic what energy is to
    physics — both are conserved quantities whose conservation prevents
    systematic error. The fiber bundle formalizes this duality by placing
    logical evidence and physical energy in the same geometric framework.
    *(hypothesis, connecting to later 2026 papers)*
