# Claim inventory — NFTs and the Gamification of the Economy

Source: Ben Goertzel, "NFTs and the Gamification of the Economy,"
Eurykosmotron, 2022-03-07.

## Epistemic-status key

- **source-paraphrase**: directly stated or closely implied in the article.
- **inferred**: not stated verbatim but follows from the article's logic.
- **hypothesis**: proposed by the article as a conjecture.
- **hyperseed-interpretation**: formalized within the Hyperseed ontology.

### Core claims from the article

1. **Gamification megatrend.** Economic activity is increasingly structured
   like games — collectibles, achievements, social signaling. NFTs
   crystallize this trend. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Digital ownership revolution.** NFTs solve digital ownership — proving
   unique ownership of digital items where copies are trivial. This creates
   new economic possibilities. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Social signaling.** Much NFT value derives from social signaling —
   membership, taste, status. This is a fundamental human drive extended
   into digital space. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Creator empowerment.** NFTs enable direct creator monetization without
   intermediaries, with smart-contract royalties and direct collector
   relationships. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Post-scarcity transition.** Gamified economics is transitional toward
   post-scarcity — when material needs are met, activity shifts to
   experience, meaning, and play. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Speculative excess ≠ invalid innovation.** NFT market bubbles don't
   invalidate the underlying structural innovation, just as the dot-com
   bubble didn't invalidate the web. *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **AI-generated content.** NFTs provide ownership/attribution mechanisms
   for AI-created content — relevant for AGI economy. *(inferred)*
