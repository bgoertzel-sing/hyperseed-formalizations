# Provably Safe AGI is Potentially a Very Dangerous Concept

**Source:** [bengoertzel.substack.com/p/provably-safe-agi-is-potentially](https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/provably-safe-agi-is-potentially)
**Date:** 2024-05-21

## Summary

Argues that the pursuit of 'provably safe' AGI is misguided and potentially dangerous: formal verification of safety properties in systems as complex as AGI is intractable, and the false confidence it provides could be worse than acknowledging uncertainty. Advocates for robust safety through architecture and values rather than formal proofs.

## Hyperseed Ontology Interpretation

the fiber of safety assurance over the base of system complexity has a fundamental curvature that prevents global flat sections (proofs); the d-calculus shows that parallel transport of safety properties requires adaptive connections, not rigid formal ones
