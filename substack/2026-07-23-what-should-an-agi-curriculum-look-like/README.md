## Summary

Announces the first accredited AGI degree program (MS in AGI at CIHS) and
uses it to articulate what AGI education should contain at this historical
juncture.

### Core argument

1. **Three core courses = three levels.** Mind (conceptual/philosophical),
   Brain (neural/computational substrate), Experience (phenomenology,
   consciousness, what-it's-like). AGI education requires all three levels,
   not just engineering.

2. **Deep thinking vs. vibe coding.** In an era when it's easy to spin up
   code and ask ChatGPT, many people don't bother to think deeply. But
   building actual AGI requires hard thinking — minor variations on what's
   been done before won't get you there.

3. **Beneficial AGI beneath the guardrails.** AI ethics isn't fundamentally
   about guardrails or prompt rules. The deeper questions: What does it mean
   for an AGI to have a value? What does it mean for a human-AI collective
   to evolve with an evolving value system?

4. **Attention as precious commodity.** In the AGI era, attention is among
   the most precious commodities. A degree program focuses a group's
   attention on a topic and on each other — that's its unique value.

5. **Open curriculum + enrolled cohort.** Most curriculum released freely;
   the unique value of enrollment is focused discussion, expert assessment,
   and peer interaction.

## Hyperseed ontology interpretation

### Three levels as fiber decomposition

AGI understanding requires multiple independent fibers:

- **Mind fiber.** Conceptual/philosophical level — what is intelligence?
  What are the design principles? What are the goals and values? This
  fiber contains the abstract architecture of AGI.

- **Brain fiber.** Neural/computational substrate — how do you actually
  build it? What are the algorithms, the training procedures, the
  engineering constraints? This fiber contains the implementation.

- **Experience fiber.** Phenomenology, consciousness, what-it's-like —
  what does it mean for an AGI to have experiences? How do you design
  for beneficial experience? This fiber contains the first-person
  perspective.

- **Anti-scalar-collapse.** Collapsing AGI understanding to any single
  fiber (just philosophy, just engineering, just phenomenology) loses
  essential structure. The AGI understanding bundle requires all three
  fibers — same multi-dimensional principle as the scarce properties
  in the K3 article.

### Deep thinking vs vibe coding as directed-type depth

Two different levels of the directed type:

- **Vibe coding = 0-cell operations.** Surface pattern matching from the
  training distribution. Operates at the level of immediate response —
  what token comes next? What API call solves this? No directed history,
  no novel trajectory, no reflection.

- **Deep thinking = 1-cells and higher.** Novel reasoning trajectories
  (1-cells: directed paths through problem space), reflection on
  reasoning (2-cells: transformations between reasoning paths), and
  genuine understanding (higher cells: the full directed type of
  comprehension).

- **Building AGI requires higher cells.** Minor variations on existing
  approaches (staying near the 0-cell surface) won't produce AGI.
  Building AGI requires navigating novel 1-cells (new reasoning paths)
  and evaluating them from the 2-cell level (which paths are promising?).

### Value possession as fiber integration

The deep ethics question from Goals That Grow Back:

- **Guardrails = section-level constraint.** External rules imposed on
  the system — a specific section of the ethics fiber. Can be overridden
  by changing the base point (new context, new pressure, jailbreak).

- **Value possession = fiber-level property.** The value is integrated
  into the system's fiber structure — it's part of how the system
  processes information, not an external constraint on its outputs.
  Preserved under base transformations.

- **Education's role.** Deep AGI education aims at fiber-level
  understanding (genuine comprehension) rather than section-level
  knowledge (memorized facts and procedures).

### Attention as parallel transport budget

Attention is the resource that enables genuine understanding:

- **Focused attention = transport along a directed path.** Understanding
  a topic requires transporting your epistemic state along a directed
  path through the topic's structure. This transport requires
  computational budget (attention, time, effort).

- **Degree program = organized transport.** The program organizes a
  cohort to transport together along curated paths — focused discussion,
  expert guidance, peer interaction. The paths are curated (curriculum)
  but the transport is collaborative.

- **Attention scarcity.** In the AGI era, the bottleneck isn't information
  (abundant) but attention (scarce). The degree program's value is
  allocating attention — the precious transport budget — to the right
  paths.

### d-calculus connection (Hyperseed v2)

- **Fiber decomposition curvature.** The curvature between the three
  fibers (Mind, Brain, Experience):

  ||F_∇(Mind, Brain)|| = independence of conceptual and implementation
    understanding
  ||F_∇(Mind, Experience)|| = independence of conceptual and
    phenomenological understanding
  ||F_∇(Brain, Experience)|| = independence of implementation and
    phenomenological understanding

  High curvature between pairs: the fibers are genuinely independent
  (understanding one doesn't give you the others — you need all three).
  This high inter-fiber curvature is the formal content of "all three
  levels required."

- **Thinking depth curvature.** The curvature of understanding as a
  function of directed-type depth:

  ||F_∇_understanding(depth)|| = understanding gain per additional
    level of directed reasoning

  High curvature: each level of deeper reasoning adds substantial
  understanding (worth investing in deep thinking). Low curvature:
  additional depth adds little (surface is sufficient — the vibe coding
  assumption, which the article argues is wrong for AGI).

- **Value integration curvature.** The curvature between guardrail-level
  and fiber-level value understanding:

  ||F_∇(guardrail, possessed)|| = depth gap between superficial and
    genuine ethical understanding

  High curvature: guardrail understanding diverges sharply from genuine
  value possession (the ethics course needs to bridge this gap). Low
  curvature: guardrails track genuine values (would mean surface ethics
  is sufficient — the article argues it isn't).

- **Attention allocation holonomy.** A loop through the learning cycle
  (attend → understand → apply → reflect → attend) produces holonomy:

  Hol_γ(Γ_learning) = understanding gain per learning cycle

  High holonomy: each cycle produces substantial new understanding
  (effective curriculum — organized transport along productive paths).
  Low holonomy: cycles produce little (ineffective curriculum — transport
  along paths that don't lead to new territory).

- **Cohort collaboration curvature.** The curvature between individual
  and collaborative understanding:

  ||F_∇(individual, collaborative)|| = collaboration value-add

  High curvature: collaborative understanding substantially exceeds
  what individuals achieve alone (the degree program's unique value —
  focused group attention). Low curvature: collaboration adds little
  (self-study suffices). The article argues this curvature is high
  for AGI education specifically because the field is so cross-
  disciplinary.
