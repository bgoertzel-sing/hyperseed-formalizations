### Critique of Anthropic and OpenAI framings

1. **Anthropic's position: slow down.** Build in the ability to slow down
   when recursive self-improvement starts. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **OpenAI's position: race defensively.** Keep racing but make sure the
   right country wins. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Both too shallow.** Both mistake a single lever for the whole mechanism.
   Avoiding catastrophe is a complex-systems architecture problem.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

### Acatastrophe > alignment

4. **"Alignment" conflates warm and cold.** Warm = shared goals. Cold =
   won't harm us. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Cold sufficient.** Cold alignment is sufficient for acatastrophe.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Three rungs.** Acatastrophe → abundance → Cosmist futures.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **Full value-fit unnecessary.** A system can diverge wildly in aesthetics
   yet not harm us. *(source-paraphrase)*

8. **Dialectic over consistency.** PLN doesn't require global consistency.
   Marx/Hegel dialectic drives progress. *(source-paraphrase)*
