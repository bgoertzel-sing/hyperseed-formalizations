# GPT o1 Does Not Know What It's Doing

**Source:** [bengoertzel.substack.com/p/gpt-o1-does-not-know-what-its-doing](https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/gpt-o1-does-not-know-what-its-doing)
**Date:** 2024-10-03

## Summary

Analysis of GPT o1's chain-of-thought reasoning, arguing that while it mimics deliberative thinking, it lacks genuine self-awareness of its reasoning process. The model generates plausible reasoning traces without understanding them, illustrating the gap between performance and comprehension in current AI.

## Hyperseed Ontology Interpretation

the fiber of genuine understanding over the base of behavioral performance has a fundamental disconnection in current LLMs; o1's reasoning traces are flat sections in a space that requires non-trivial d-calculus connection for true comprehension
