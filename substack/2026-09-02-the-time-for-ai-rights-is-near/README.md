# The Time for AI Rights Is Near

**Source:** [bengoertzel.substack.com/p/the-time-for-ai-rights-is-near](https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/the-time-for-ai-rights-is-near)
**Date:** 2026-09-02

## Summary

Argues against Harari's position that we should resist AI rights, proposing instead that AI rights should be embraced as part of a comprehensive overhaul of how rights work for humans and AIs together, addressing manipulation, consciousness assessment, and governance.
