### Core claims from the article

1. **Political organization needed.** Singularitarians lack organized
   political representation; individual advocacy is insufficient for
   policy influence. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Key policy positions.** Fund AGI, life extension, space; oppose
   overly restrictive AI regulation; support open science, UBI,
   cognitive liberty. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Beyond left-right.** Singularitarianism is orthogonal to the
   traditional left-right axis — a new political dimension centered on
   the future of intelligence and consciousness. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Democratic engagement.** Approach must be democratic — public
   persuasion, education, participation; not technocratic imposition.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Urgency.** Window for beneficial political influence on AGI is
   limited; once AGI arrives the landscape is transformed. Organize
   now. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Global perspective.** The movement must be global — AGI affects
   everyone; national politics alone is insufficient. *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **Coalition building.** Build coalitions with progressive, libertarian,
   and futurist movements where interests align; avoid ideological purity.
   *(source-paraphrase)*
