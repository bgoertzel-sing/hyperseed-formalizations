### Core claims from the article

1. **Energy as AI bottleneck.** Energy availability is the primary
   bottleneck for AI scaling. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Geopolitics of compute energy.** Control of energy infrastructure
   becomes a lever for controlling AI development. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Centralization pressure.** Energy-intensive AI favors centralization —
   only energy-rich entities can train frontier models. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Decentralization opportunity.** Distributed energy enables distributed
   AI development. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Nuclear renaissance.** AI energy demands drive renewed nuclear
   interest. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Sustainability concerns.** AI energy consumption potentially conflicts
   with climate goals. *(source-paraphrase)*
