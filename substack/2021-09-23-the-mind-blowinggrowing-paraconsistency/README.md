# The Mind-Blowing/Growing Paraconsistency of Beauty

**Source:** [bengoertzel.substack.com/p/the-mind-blowinggrowing-paraconsistency](https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/the-mind-blowinggrowing-paraconsistency)
**Date:** 2021-09-23

## Summary

Proposes that beauty is the paraconsistent unity of individuation and self-transcendence — the 'surprising fulfillment of expectations.' Connects aesthetic experience to Open-Ended Intelligence (OEI): individuation maintains pattern continuity while self-transcendence generates novelty. The tension between these is inherently paraconsistent, and the most beautiful things sit on the Pareto frontier of expectation and surprise.

## Hyperseed Ontology Interpretation

the fiber of aesthetic experience over the base of pattern-space has a paraconsistent connection where individuation and self-transcendence coexist without classical resolution; the d-calculus curvature at the beauty-frontier measures the irreducible paraconsistent tension between expectation and surprise
