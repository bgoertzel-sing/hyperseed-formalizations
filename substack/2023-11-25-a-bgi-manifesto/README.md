# A Beneficial AGI Manifesto

**Source:** [bengoertzel.substack.com/p/a-bgi-manifesto](https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/a-bgi-manifesto)
**Date:** 2023-11-25

## Summary

Comprehensive manifesto arguing for beneficial AGI development, grounded in Cosmist philosophy. Advocates for open, decentralized, cognitively diverse AGI architectures guided by compassion and growth rather than fear. Proposes ten Cosmist convictions as philosophical foundation, emphasizes the need for consciousness uplift alongside technical development, and argues that beneficial outcomes require active, cooperative human engagement rather than restrictive regulation.

## Hyperseed Ontology Interpretation

the Hyperseed fiber bundle maps the base space of AGI development trajectories to a fiber of ethical-outcome valuations; the Cosmist connection ensures parallel transport preserves beneficence across the intelligence explosion, with curvature measuring the deviation from cooperative ideals
