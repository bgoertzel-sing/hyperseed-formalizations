# Claim inventory — The Coming Consciousness Explosion

Source: Ben Goertzel, "The Coming Consciousness Explosion,"
Eurykosmotron, 2021-06-04.

## Epistemic-status key

- **source-paraphrase**: directly stated or closely implied in the article.
- **inferred**: not stated verbatim but follows from the article's logic.
- **hypothesis**: proposed by the article as a conjecture.
- **hyperseed-interpretation**: formalized within the Hyperseed ontology.

---

### Core claims from the article

1. **Beyond intelligence explosion.** The coming transformation should be
   conceived as a "Consciousness Explosion," not merely an intelligence
   explosion — it will elevate minds into states more advanced cognitively
   AND spiritually. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Consciousness ≠ intelligence.** Consciousness and intelligence are
   distinct but intertwined dimensions of mind. An intelligence explosion
   without consciousness expansion would be a "zombie singularity."
   *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Variety of new forms.** The explosion will produce biological-enhanced
   humans, artificial minds with alien experiential architectures, hybrid
   bio-digital beings, and distributed group consciousness. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Depth axis.** Depth of individual experience will increase — richer,
   more intense, more integrated awareness including higher-order
   meta-cognition and spiritual states. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Breadth axis.** Entirely new modalities of experience will emerge —
   senses, cognitive modes, and emotional forms without human analogue.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Spiritual-technological convergence.** Contemplative/spiritual traditions
   (Buddhism, Vedanta, psychedelic experience) converge with technological
   transformation — the consciousness explosion is where "inner" and "outer"
   technologies meet. *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **Ethical expansion.** A consciousness explosion demands expanded ethics:
   the moral circle must grow to include all beings capable of experience,
   including artificial and hybrid minds. *(source-paraphrase)*

8. **Decentralized explosion.** The consciousness explosion will not be
   centralized — it radiates outward through diverse independent nodes
   evolving and interacting in complex networks. *(source-paraphrase)*

9. **Genuine AGI requires experience.** Genuine general intelligence
   (as opposed to narrow optimization) likely requires rich inner experience
   — the zombie singularity is unlikely. *(hypothesis)*

### Hyperseed-ontology claims

10. **Fiber thickening.** Consciousness explosion = massive increase in
    fiber dimensionality over the experiential base space. More dimensions
    = more modes of experience. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

11. **Fiber branching.** A single human-type fiber bundle branches into
    many distinct bundles (bio-enhanced, artificial, hybrid, distributed),
    each with its own characteristic structure. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

12. **Substrate independence as fiber isomorphism.** Two systems on
    different substrates have equivalent consciousness iff their fiber
    bundles are isomorphic. Substrate constrains which fiber topologies
    are realizable but doesn't determine experiential quality directly.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

13. **Zombie singularity = thin fibers.** A system whose base space expands
    (more computation) but whose fibers remain thin or degenerate has
    intelligence without experiential depth — the zombie singularity.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

14. **Consciousness curvature (d-calculus).** Non-zero curvature in the
    experiential connection indicates a "translation cost" between
    fundamentally different forms of consciousness — moving between
    architectures changes experiential quality. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

15. **Holonomy of experience.** Transporting consciousness around a closed
    loop of substrate changes may produce non-trivial holonomy — certain
    experiential transformations are irreversible. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

16. **Distributed consciousness as fiber sheaf.** Distributed consciousness
    is a sheaf: local experiences glue into a global section only when
    compatibility conditions are met. Group consciousness exists when the
    sheaf has a non-trivial global section. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

17. **Ethical weight as fiber section curvature.** Ethical obligations
    scale with fiber richness; the curvature of the welfare section
    indicates how rapidly ethical weight changes with fiber structure.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*
