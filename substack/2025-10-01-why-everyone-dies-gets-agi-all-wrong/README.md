# Why 'Everyone Dies' Gets AGI All Wrong

**Source:** [bengoertzel.substack.com/p/why-everyone-dies-gets-agi-all-wrong](https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/why-everyone-dies-gets-agi-all-wrong)
**Date:** 2025-10-01

## Summary

Refutes the 'AI doom' narrative that AGI inevitably leads to human extinction. Argues the doom case rests on flawed assumptions about goal-directed optimization, instrumental convergence, and the nature of superintelligent motivation, and that beneficial outcomes are both possible and more likely with the right approach.

## Hyperseed Ontology Interpretation

the fiber of existential outcome over the base of AGI architecture has been incorrectly modeled as a single doom-section; the actual bundle has multiple viable sections, and the d-calculus connection shows that beneficial architectures can maintain safety under parallel transport
