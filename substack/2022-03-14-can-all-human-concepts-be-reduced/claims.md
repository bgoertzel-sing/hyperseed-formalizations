### Core claims from the article

1. **Semantic primitives exist.** A small set of primitive concepts (~65
   NSM primes) may underlie all human concepts via recursive composition.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Practical utility.** Even approximate primitive-sets are useful for
   AGI knowledge representation and cross-cultural communication.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Embodiment grounding.** Some primitives are grounded in sensorimotor
   experience; abstract primitives may be metaphorical extensions.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Recursive composition.** Complex concepts are recursive compositions
   of simpler ones. The question is whether recursion bottoms out.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **CogPrime/Hyperon application.** Hyperon's AtomSpace needs tractable
   concept grounding; primitives offer one approach. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Limits of reduction.** Some concepts (qualia, consciousness,
   mathematical primitives) may resist decomposition. *(source-paraphrase)*
