Epstein as revealed in the Epstein files. Documents the funding relationship

## Hyperseed ontology interpretation

### Provenance as fiber history

In the Hyperseed framework, provenance is the history of how fiber was
created — the path through base space that produced the current fiber:

- **Fiber provenance.** Every fiber element has a provenance — the
  history of how it was created, funded, motivated, and developed.
  The fiber (AGI research) has a provenance trail that includes the
  base points (funding sources, collaborators, institutions) through
  which it was developed.

- **Provenance indelibility.** Provenance cannot be retroactively
  cleaned — the path through base space that created the fiber is
  a historical fact. The fiber's history includes all base points
  traversed, even ones that are later revealed to be problematic.

- **Provenance transparency.** Honest documentation of provenance
  is epistemic integrity — the fiber's history must be accurately
  reported, not sanitized. This article is itself an exercise in
  provenance transparency.

### Due diligence as base verification

Due diligence is verifying the base before building fiber on it:

- **Base verification.** Before accepting funding (connecting fiber
  to a base point), the researcher should verify the base point's
  integrity — is the funding source legitimate, ethical, aligned?

- **Verification failure.** Due diligence failure is connecting fiber
  to an unverified base point. The fiber itself may be perfectly good
  (the research is valid) but its provenance is tainted by connection
  to a problematic base point.

- **Fiber-base separation.** The quality of fiber (research quality)
  is logically independent of base-point quality (funding source
  ethics). Good fiber can have bad provenance and vice versa. But
  provenance matters for trust — the social fiber bundle requires
  clean provenance for full transport into communal knowledge.

### d-calculus connection (Hyperseed v2)

- **Provenance curvature.** The curvature of the provenance connection
  measures how much the funding source affects the research direction:

  ||F_∇_provenance|| = influence of funding source on research direction

  Zero curvature: funding has no directional influence (ideal).
  Non-zero curvature: funding biases research direction (problematic).
  In this case, Goertzel argues the curvature was near zero — the
  Epstein funding didn't influence research direction.

- **Trust connection suppression.** When provenance is revealed to
  include problematic base points, the trust connection between the
  researcher and the community is suppressed:

  Γ_trust(researcher → community) decreases when provenance is tainted

  Transparency (this article) works to restore the trust connection
  by providing complete provenance information, allowing the community
  to evaluate the fiber on its merits rather than suppress it due to
  provenance concerns.

- **Provenance holonomy.** Traversing the provenance loop (create →
  fund → produce → reveal → reckon) produces holonomy:

  Hol_γ(Γ_provenance) ≠ id → the researcher is changed by the
  reckoning

  The holonomy is the moral and epistemic growth from confronting
  the provenance honestly. Avoiding the loop (hiding provenance)
  prevents the holonomy — no growth from an unconfronted history.
