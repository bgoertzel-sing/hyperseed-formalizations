# Claim inventory — The Rise of the Closed-Ended Quasi-AGI

Source: Ben Goertzel, "The Rise of the Closed-Ended Quasi-AGI,"
Eurykosmotron, 2022-08-24.

### Core claims from the article

1. **Closed-ended quasi-AGI.** LLMs exhibit broad competence confined to
   training distribution — "quasi-AGI" (broad) but "closed-ended" (bounded).
   They interpolate impressively but don't extrapolate genuinely.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Impressive within bounds.** The competence is real and practically
   useful within the training distribution. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Missing open-endedness.** True AGI requires handling genuinely novel
   situations, creating new concepts, modifying own architecture, and
   transferring to unprecedented domains. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Dangerous conflation.** Treating quasi-AGI as true AGI leads to false
   confidence, wrong policy, misallocated research, and existential risk
   distortion. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Hybrid path.** Path to true AGI: use quasi-AGI as components within
   larger open-ended systems with explicit reasoning, planning, and
   self-modification. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Historical pattern.** Each AI breakthrough is initially mistaken for
   AGI, then understood as a valuable but limited component. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Hyperseed-ontology claims

7. **Bounded fiber.** Quasi-AGI has fixed fiber: F_quasi(b,t) ⊆ F_training
   for all b, t. The system navigates within but cannot extend the fiber
   space. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

8. **Growing fiber.** True AGI has growing fiber: d/dt dim(F(b,t)) > 0.
   New fiber dimensions emerge autopoietically in response to novelty.
   *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

9. **Interpolation = convex hull.** Quasi-AGI moves within the convex hull
   of training fibers; impressive but bounded. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

10. **Extrapolation = fiber growth.** True AGI moves beyond the convex hull
    via autopoietic fiber creation. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

11. **Conflation = fiber-type error.** Treating bounded fiber as growing
    fiber; indistinguishable within training distribution, divergent at
    boundary. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

12. **Fiber growth rate (d-calculus).** ∂F/∂t is a section of the tangent
    bundle to the fiber bundle. Quasi-AGI: ∂F/∂t = 0. True AGI: ∂F/∂t > 0.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

13. **Boundary curvature (d-calculus).** The curvature of the F_training
    boundary determines competence drop-off sharpness. High = cliff;
    low = gradual degradation. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

14. **Autopoietic connection (d-calculus).** True AGI has an evolving
    connection — new transport paths emerge over time. Quasi-AGI has static
    connection fixed at training. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

15. **Novelty curvature (d-calculus).** Fiber bundle curvature at base point
    b measures novelty relative to training. Quasi-AGI fails at high-novelty
    points; true AGI adapts. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*
