   *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **AI regulation parallel.** Safety advocates (Baptists) and Big Tech
   (Bootleggers) support the same regulations. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Regulatory capture dynamics.** Regulations disproportionately burden
   small players and open-source. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Not a conspiracy.** Structural dynamic, not coordinated conspiracy.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Real safety risk.** Concentration itself is a safety risk; regulations
   may reduce safety by reducing diversity. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Decentralized alternative.** Open standards, interoperability,
   anti-monopoly regulation. *(source-paraphrase)*
