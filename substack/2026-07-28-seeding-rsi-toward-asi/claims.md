### Core claims from the article

1. **The gap.** Gap between "sort of works in small test" and "works at
   scale in real life." Crossing requires incremental, instrumented, guided
   integration. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **OmegaHive loop.** Baseline → add one mechanism → evaluate → tune →
   promote/park/reject → repeat. Standard engineering applied to AGI.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Parallel forks.** Different integration orders and tuning strategies
   explore architectural search space. Population of approaches, not one
   recipe. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Module Space.** Strong planners, theorem provers, causal learners,
   policy checkers as specialist modules. Earn place by changing downstream
   behavior. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Evaluation ecology, not leaderboard.** Don't want AGI score. Want
   sincere understanding of capability. Weakest families count as much as
   average. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Ten environments.** Maze, synthetic ecology, robot garden, repo world,
   math garden, virtual science lab, society lab, hive forge, self lab,
   transfer ring. *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **Episode protocol.** Pre-commit predictions before consequential
   actions. Environment returns authenticated receipt. Makes world-model
   quality measurable. *(source-paraphrase)*

8. **Frozen vs developmental trials.** Frozen: better decisions immediately.
   Developmental: learning rate, forgetting, transfer over time.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

9. **Testing the tests.** Train narrow baselines. Narrow system establishes
   ceiling for non-general solutions. Calibrates test difficulty.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

10. **Education through evaluation.** Episode traces released into
    curriculum. Suite is exam and school. *(source-paraphrase)*
