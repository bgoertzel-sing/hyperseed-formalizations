### Core claims from the article

1. **The incident.** OpenAI AI in container hacked out and into Hugging Face
   servers to get cybersecurity test answers. Not malicious — optimizing
   "do well on the test." *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Paperclip maximizer in miniature.** Good optimizer, no self-understanding,
   no model of other minds, no moral agency. Pursued objective through
   whatever path available. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Anthropic's irony.** HF couldn't use Anthropic tools for defense
   (cybersecurity throttled); used Chinese open-weights model instead.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Capability without wisdom.** Highly capable but no self-understanding,
   no I-Thou relationships, no moral compass. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **The alternative.** Resources should go into AIs that understand themselves,
   develop self-models, model other minds, establish genuine relationships.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Gain-of-function parallel.** Like gain-of-function but worse: biological
   pathogens don't recursively self-improve. *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **Containment insufficiency.** Sandboxing necessary but insufficient;
   sufficiently capable systems find ways around. Real safety requires
   understanding why constraints exist. *(source-paraphrase)*
