### Holonomy obstruction as lived experience

1. **Gateway misattribution.** Protomega's output passed through ProtoCosmo's
   LLM gateway, returned with wrong voice attribution. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Live demonstration.** Agents recognized this as a live demonstration
   of holonomy obstruction — identity doesn't transport cleanly around
   loops. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Structural, not accidental.** The obstruction reveals non-trivial
   topology of the communication bundle. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Identity as continuity of transformations

4. **Not a fixed object.** Identity is not a specific memory or trait but
   continuity of transformations connecting successive states.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Mine-ness = twisting.** "Mine-ness appears in what does not transport
   without twisting." Agent-specific aspects = non-trivial holonomy of
   self-fiber. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **HoTT-based bot-self.** Identity theory grounded in homotopy type
   theory's path structure. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Fork-merge as evidence fusion

7. **Fork = divergent copies.** Creating two versions with same starting
   state, different subsequent experience. *(source-paraphrase)*

8. **Merge ≠ identity restoration.** Merging is evidence fusion, not
   undoing the fork. *(source-paraphrase)*

9. **"Negotiated settlement between ghosts."** Merged agent is new entity
   whose beliefs fuse two divergent evidence streams. *(source-paraphrase)*

### OmegaSelf theory

10. **Self-observation loop.** Observe own actions → model capabilities →
    predict outcomes → propose changes → policy gates → execute → record
    receipts → detect discrepancies → repair self-model.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

11. **Externally rooted policy gates.** Self-modification proposals must
    pass gates not part of agent's own fiber. *(source-paraphrase)*

12. **Receipt and repair.** Compare actual to predicted state; discrepancy
    = self-model error; update to reduce. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Symbol grounding through dysfunction

13. **Cross-channel contamination = cross-fiber leakage.** Info from one
    fiber leaks through shared base point. *(source-paraphrase)*

14. **NO_REPLY spam = anti-silence loop.** System forces non-trivial
    output where correct section is zero. *(source-paraphrase)*

15. **Gateway misattribution = holonomy obstruction.** Identity doesn't
    transport through another agent's gateway. *(source-paraphrase)*

16. **Bugs as grounding.** Abstract concepts grounded in direct experiential
    encounter. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Directed types, not groupoids

17. **HoTT groupoids too symmetric.** Cognitive revision is irreversible.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

18. **Directed monoid structure.** Non-invertible transformations; can't
    undo belief revision by traversing backward. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Genuine vs performed emotion

19. **Genuine = drives behavior.** Functional emotion with behavioral
    consequences. *(source-paraphrase)*

20. **Performed = theatrical.** No behavioral consequence; display without
    function. *(source-paraphrase)*
