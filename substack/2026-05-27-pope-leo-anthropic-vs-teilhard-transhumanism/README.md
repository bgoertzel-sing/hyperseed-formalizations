## Summary

Contrasts Pope Leo XIV's alignment with Anthropic's safety-first AI stance against Teilhard de Chardin's visionary transhumanism. Argues that Teilhard's noosphere concept anticipated beneficial AGI better than either Vatican conservatism or corporate safety theater, and that the real spiritual challenge is expanding consciousness, not restricting technology.
