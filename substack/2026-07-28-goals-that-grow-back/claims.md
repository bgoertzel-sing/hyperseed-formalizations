### Core claims from the article

1. **Delete the Sentence.** Remove explicit goal statement — for most AI
   systems, goal vanishes. For humans, goal regenerates from woven structure.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Goal adoption vs possession.** Adoption = externally supplied, fragile.
   Possession = regenerative, structurally embedded. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Goal possession as attractor.** Possessed goal = attractor of mind's
   dynamics. Delete representation → cognitive processes regenerate
   equivalent. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Regenerative depth.** Measurable number: how much damage can the value
   absorb and still bounce back. Experimentally testable prediction.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Gate alone fails.** Checkpoint for deliberate self-modifications fails
   against ordinary wear. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Weave alone fails.** Web of memories and connections wrecked by one
   approved modification too big. *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **Gate and weave need each other.** Complementary: gate protects weave
   from catastrophic mod; weave protects from gradual erosion.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

8. **Goal regeneration as funded race.** Repair competes for attention.
   Adversary can kill goal by starving repair. *(source-paraphrase)*

9. **Minimum repair guarantee.** Repair resources must be guaranteed, not
   left to market competition. *(source-paraphrase)*

10. **Transformative experience.** Joy with bittersweet tinge — joy at new
    horizons (warranted if real continuity), bittersweetness for old self.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

11. **Ship of Theseus.** What persists = pattern + repair processes, not
    parts or arrangement. *(source-paraphrase)*
