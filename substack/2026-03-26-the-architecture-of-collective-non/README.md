## Summary

Proposes architectural principles for collective intelligence systems that embody non-ego: shared attention without central control, transparent reasoning without proprietary opacity, and compassionate coordination without dominance hierarchies.

the fiber bundle of collective cognition has individual agents as base and shared understanding as fiber; the non-ego connection ensures parallel transport preserves collective coherence without requiring a central controller
