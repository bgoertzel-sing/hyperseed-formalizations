# Claim inventory — The Emerging Power of Decentralization

Source: Ben Goertzel, "The Emerging Power of Decentralization,"
Eurykosmotron, 2021-05-30.

- **inferred**: not stated verbatim but follows from the article's logic.
- **hypothesis**: proposed by the article as a conjecture.

### Core claims from the article

1. **Decentralization megatrend.** The movement from centralized to
   decentralized organization is a fundamental structural transformation
   accelerating across all domains. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Blockchain as trustless coordination.** Blockchain provides trustless
   coordination infrastructure, removing the traditional justification for
   centralization (the need for a trusted coordinator). *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Decentralized AI imperative.** AGI development must be decentralized
   to avoid catastrophic concentration of power. SingularityNET embodies
   this vision. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Governance innovation.** DAOs, liquid democracy, and quadratic voting
   offer governance structures more responsive, inclusive, and capture-
   resistant than traditional hierarchies. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Resilience through redundancy.** Decentralized systems have no single
   point of failure — inherently more resilient. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Coordination without control.** Coordination does not require control —
   protocols, incentives, and shared standards can replace hierarchical
   command. *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **Cultural shift.** Decentralization is also a cultural transformation:
   from authority-deference to peer collaboration, proprietary to open,
   permission-based to permissionless. *(source-paraphrase)*

8. **SingularityNET model.** A decentralized marketplace of AI services,
   where no single entity controls the network, provides a viable model
   for safe AGI development. *(source-paraphrase)*
