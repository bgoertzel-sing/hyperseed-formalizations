# Claim inventory — The General Theory of General Intelligence

Source: Ben Goertzel, "The General Theory of General Intelligence,"
Eurykosmotron, 2021-06-02 (introducing arXiv:2103.15100).

- **source-paraphrase**: directly stated or closely implied in the article.
- **inferred**: not stated verbatim but follows from the article's logic.

### Core claims from the article

1. **Pragmatic patternism.** Intelligence is the ability to achieve complex
   goals in complex environments; the key primitive is *pattern* — a
   representation simpler than what it represents. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Structural property.** General intelligence is a structural property of
   systems, not a specific algorithm — it's about the organization of
   computation, not its substrate. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Category-theoretic formalization.** Intelligence can be formalized as
   functors between environment and action categories; learning as natural
   transformations between functors; self-modification as endofunctors on
   the intelligence category. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Metatransparency.** General intelligence requires metatransparency —
   the ability to inspect and modify one's own cognitive processes, and to
   understand *why* modifications are made. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Goal diversity and transfer.** Generality means effectiveness across
   diverse goals; transfer learning is essential — patterns learned in one
   domain must apply in others. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Cognitive synergy.** Intelligence emerges from cognitive synergy —
   multiple specialized processes (attention, perception, reasoning, memory,
   learning, action) interacting to produce capabilities none has alone.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **CogPrime/Hyperon.** OpenCog/Hyperon implements these principles via
   multiple interacting algorithms (PLN, MOSES, pattern mining, deep
   learning) in a shared hypergraph (AtomSpace). *(source-paraphrase)*

8. **Pattern = compressed representation.** A pattern is a representation
   that is simpler (shorter description length) than what it represents,
   relative to a given reference computational model. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Hyperseed-ontology claims

9. **Intelligence = fiber richness.** General intelligence corresponds to
   thick, diverse fiber structure over the environmental base space. Thick
   fibers = many available response patterns per environmental state.
   *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

10. **Fiber diversity = cognitive synergy.** Different fiber types correspond
    to different cognitive modalities; cognitive synergy = emergent fiber
    structure from fiber-type interaction. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

11. **Fiber coverage = generality.** AGI has fibers over a large portion of
    the environment base space; narrow AI has fibers over a small region.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

12. **Functor = fiber bundle morphism.** The intelligence functor F: E → A
    is a fiber bundle morphism mapping environmental fibers to action fibers.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

13. **Learning = fiber homotopy.** Natural transformation η: F₁ ⟹ F₂ is
    a fiber bundle homotopy — continuous deformation of the intelligence
    morphism. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

14. **Self-modification = fiber endomorphism.** The system reshapes its own
    fiber structure via endomorphisms. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

15. **Metatransparency = meta-fiber.** The system has a fiber section over
    its own fiber bundle — understanding of its own fiber structure.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

16. **Transfer learning = low-curvature connection (d-calculus).** Transfer
    succeeds when the intelligence connection has low curvature — patterns
    transport well between domains. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

17. **Cognitive synergy curvature.** Non-zero curvature indicates non-trivial
    interaction between cognitive modalities — the whole exceeds the sum.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

18. **Self-modification holonomy.** Iterating self-modification around a loop
    of tasks produces holonomy; non-trivial holonomy = genuine learning,
    trivial = mere cycling. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

19. **Pattern = flat section.** A pattern is a flat section of the
    intelligence bundle; pattern recognition = constructing flat sections;
    pattern creation = extending them to new base-space regions.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*
