## Summary

A manifesto for Beneficial General Intelligence (BGI). Argues that HLAGI
is within reach, that ASI will follow quickly via intelligence explosion,
and that the key question is whether AGI development is guided by
beneficial values. Contrasts the BGI approach (open, decentralized,
value-aligned) with corporate capture and regulatory restriction.

### Core argument

1. **HLAGI is near.** Clear technical problems with solid-looking routes
   to solve them. Once reached, ASI probably not far behind via
   self-improvement.

2. **Intelligence explosion.** An AGI with human-level scientific competence
   can study and improve itself, triggering rapid "Singularity."

3. **Beneficial vs dangerous.** The question is not whether AGI will be
   built, but whether it will be beneficial — depends on who builds it
   and with what values.

4. **Open development.** Open-source, decentralized development is safer
   than corporate capture — transparency enables scrutiny.

5. **Against restriction.** Regulatory restriction risks concentrating AGI
   in the hands of a few powerful actors, which is the actual danger.

6. **Value alignment through architecture.** BGI through architectural
   design, not bolted-on constraints — values emerge from the system's
   structure, not from external rules imposed on it.

### Connection to other Goertzel work

- **Beneficial AGI Summit:** The manifesto is the intellectual foundation

- **Superhuman superminds:** Structural benevolence argument supports

## Hyperseed ontology interpretation

### BGI as beneficial fiber architecture

In the Hyperseed framework, BGI is AGI with fiber architecture oriented
toward benefit:

- **Beneficial fiber.** BGI fiber is structured so that capability growth
  is coupled to flourishing growth. The fiber's connection ensures that
  increasing capability (thicker fiber) necessarily increases benefit
  (better-aligned fiber).

- **Architectural vs bolted-on.** Bolted-on alignment is a separate
  constraint fiber layered on top of capability fiber — it can be
  peeled off, bypassed, or become misaligned. Architectural alignment
  is integral to the capability fiber itself — the capability IS
  beneficial because the fiber structure ensures it.

- **Fiber integrity.** BGI requires fiber integrity — the fiber's
  beneficial structure must be preserved under self-modification.
  The system must be able to improve itself without destroying the
  beneficial properties of its fiber architecture.

### Intelligence explosion as fiber autocatalysis

The intelligence explosion is fiber autocatalysis — fiber that
modifies itself to accelerate its own growth:

- **Self-modification capability.** At HLAGI, the system's fiber
  includes models of itself — the fiber can reason about and modify
  its own structure. This is fiber self-reference reaching the level
  of self-modification.

- **Autocatalytic growth.** Each self-modification produces more
  capable fiber, which produces better self-modifications, which
  produces even more capable fiber. The growth is autocatalytic —
  the rate depends on the current state.

- **Beneficial autocatalysis.** For BGI, the autocatalytic growth
  must preserve beneficial fiber properties. The self-modification
  capability must be constrained (architecturally, not externally)
  to maintain the fiber's beneficial orientation.

### Open development as fiber transparency

Open-source development means the fiber is inspectable and
modifiable by the community:

- **Transparent fiber.** Open development makes the fiber (code,
  architecture, training data, values) transparent — anyone can
  inspect the fiber structure and verify its properties.

- **Community verification.** Transparency enables community
  verification — many observers can check that the fiber is
  genuinely beneficial, not just claimed to be.

- **Diversity through openness.** Open development enables
  diverse fiber variants — different groups can adapt the base
  fiber to different contexts, maintaining portfolio diversity.

### Corporate capture as fiber monopoly

Concentration of AGI development is fiber monopoly:

- **Single fiber dominance.** Corporate capture means one fiber
  structure (one company's AGI) dominates — reducing diversity,
  concentrating power, and eliminating competitive pressure for
  beneficence.

- **Regulatory capture.** Regulation that restricts AGI development
  to a few players is fiber monopoly by another name — it
  concentrates fiber creation in approved hands.

### d-calculus connection (Hyperseed v2)

- **Beneficial fiber curvature.** The curvature of BGI fiber measures
  how well capability and benefit are coupled:

  ||F_∇_BGI|| ≈ 0 → perfect coupling (capability IS benefit)
  ||F_∇_BGI|| > 0 → imperfect coupling (capability can diverge from benefit)

  Architectural alignment minimizes this curvature; bolted-on alignment
  leaves it high (the bolt can slip, introducing curvature at the
  attachment point).

- **Autocatalytic holonomy.** Each self-improvement cycle produces
  holonomy in the BGI fiber bundle:

  Hol_γ(Γ_self-modify) ≠ id → system changes through self-modification

  For BGI, the holonomy must preserve the beneficial fiber subspace:
  Hol_γ maps beneficial fiber to beneficial fiber. This is the
  formal content of "value alignment under self-modification."

- **Transparency gradient.** The gradient ∇_transparent in development
  fiber space points toward greater inspectability:

  ∇_transparent = ∇(inspectability of fiber)

  Open development follows this gradient; corporate capture moves
  against it. The manifesto argues that following the transparency
  gradient is both safer and more effective.

- **Monopoly curvature.** Fiber monopoly produces high curvature at
  the monopoly boundary — a sharp division between the monopolist's
  fiber and everything else:

  ||F_∇_monopoly|| → ∞ at the boundary of the monopolist's control

  This curvature is dangerous because it concentrates power at a
  singular point. Diverse, open development produces low, distributed
  curvature — no sharp boundaries, no concentration points.

- **Manifesto as fiber declaration.** The manifesto itself is a fiber
  declaration — it defines the target fiber structure for BGI
  development. It's not just describing a desired future but actively
  shaping the fiber by defining what "beneficial" means and how to
  achieve it. The manifesto is a connection — it connects current
  development fiber to the target beneficial fiber.
