# Symbolic Ruminations on Non-Symbolic Consciousness

**Source:** [bengoertzel.substack.com/p/symbolic-ruminations-on-non-symbolic](https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/symbolic-ruminations-on-non-symbolic)
**Date:** 2022-08-06

## Summary

Explores Jeffery Martin's concept of Persistent Non-Symbolic Experience (PNSE) through the lens of cognitive architecture. Argues that 'non-symbolic' consciousness is not the absence of symbols but a state where symbols remain transparent to what they represent, never supplanting direct experience. Connects this to AGI design: minds that maintain awareness of the gap between symbol and referent avoid pathological attachment loops.

## Hyperseed Ontology Interpretation

the fiber of symbolic representation over the base of direct experience has a natural connection in PNSE where parallel transport keeps symbols grounded; pathological attachment is curvature accumulation — the d-calculus derivative of attachment with respect to abstraction depth measures the risk of symbol-referent decoupling
