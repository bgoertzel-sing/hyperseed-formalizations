### Core claims from the article

1. **Circular financing is real but overdetermined.** Nvidia→hyperscalers→
   cloud→startups→Nvidia is real. Cause is Nash equilibrium of a two-player
   race, not stupidity. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Geopolitical prisoner's dilemma.** US and China locked in winner-take-most
   race. Both all-in is Nash; both sizing down unstable. Near-total prize
   dissipation predicted. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Fast scenario (no deAGI).** HLAGI-2029: bet pays off but winners may
   be expropriated; nasty 2027-2028 squeeze. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Moderate scenario (no deAGI).** HLAGI-2035: refunding gate falls before
   payoff. Classic infrastructure crash. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Pessimist scenario (no deAGI).** Plateau: permanent shortfall, crash,
   blame politics. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Fast + deAGI.** Unownable prize defuses preemption logic.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **Moderate + deAGI.** Crash feeds network (fire-sale hardware jumps
   capacity). *(source-paraphrase)*

8. **Pessimist + deAGI.** Network is minimum-cost producer, no debt service.
   Fractional-Kelly survives what Full Kelly cannot. *(source-paraphrase)*

9. **Financial crisis is uninformative.** Crash is modal across all timelines.
   Leverage adjudicates timing, not terminus. *(source-paraphrase)*

10. **Fractional Kelly vs Full Kelly.** States went Full Kelly; decentralized
    = fractional-Kelly, no ruin branch. *(source-paraphrase)*
