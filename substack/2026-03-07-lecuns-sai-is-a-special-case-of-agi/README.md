# LeCun's SAI Is a Special Case of AGI, Not an Alternative

**Source:** [bengoertzel.substack.com/p/lecuns-sai-is-a-special-case-of-agi](https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/lecuns-sai-is-a-special-case-of-agi)
**Date:** 2026-03-07

## Summary

Argues that LeCun's 'Superhuman Adaptable Intelligence' (SAI) concept is a subset of AGI rather than an alternative to it. SAI's emphasis on world models and planning is correct but incomplete without explicit reasoning, meta-cognition, and the broader cognitive architecture that AGI requires.

## Hyperseed Ontology Interpretation

the fiber bundle of cognitive capabilities has SAI as a local section covering perception and planning, while AGI requires the full global structure including reasoning and meta-cognition fibers; the d-calculus connection shows SAI is a flat section in a curved space
