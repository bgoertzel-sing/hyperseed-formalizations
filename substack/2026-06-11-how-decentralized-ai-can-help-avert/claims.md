### Core claims from the article

1. **Desktop synthesis + AI + competence = danger.** The capability
   cannot be contained at the information layer. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **"Stop the models" doesn't work.** Capability is diffusing; open
   weights are loose; determined actors don't need the frontier.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Cryptographic laterality doesn't apply.** The dangerous thing is
   information, not a networked mind — small, copyable, un-recallable.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Gate the atoms, not the bits.** Physical reagents are the leverage
   point. Gate the recurring consumable, not the one-time box.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Biology ≠ cybersecurity.** Can't patch a human body. Vaccines slow,
   partial, limited reach. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Decentralized verification > central authority.** Central database =
   single point of failure, honeypot, political non-starter.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **Device identity.** Cryptographic key sealed in chip. Anyone can
   verify, nobody owns. *(source-paraphrase)*

8. **Operator credentials.** Accreditation, training, clean history.
   Verifiable without central registry. *(source-paraphrase)*

9. **Scoped reputation.** Multi-dimensional, fading, delegable. Not a
   single trust score. *(source-paraphrase)*

10. **Neural-symbolic screening.** LLM pattern recognition + PLN uncertain
    reasoning about intent, context, provenance. *(source-paraphrase)*

11. **Distributed screening nodes.** No single entity holds the master
    watchlist. *(source-paraphrase)*

12. **Distributed biosurveillance.** Environmental DNA, wastewater,
    clinical anomaly detection. Immune system, not watchtower.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

    full coverage. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*
