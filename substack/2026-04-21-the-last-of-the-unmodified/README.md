# The Last of the Unmodified

**Source:** [bengoertzel.substack.com/p/the-last-of-the-unmodified](https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/the-last-of-the-unmodified)
**Date:** 2026-04-21

## Summary

Speculative exploration of what it will be like to be among the last generation of unmodified humans as cognitive enhancement and brain-computer interfaces become widespread. Argues for respect and dignity for the unmodified while celebrating the expansion of cognitive possibilities.

## Hyperseed Ontology Interpretation

the fiber of cognitive capability over the base of social identity undergoes a phase transition at the enhancement frontier; the d-calculus connection must be extended smoothly to preserve dignity across the modified/unmodified boundary
