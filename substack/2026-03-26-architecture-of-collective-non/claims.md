### Core claims from the article

1. **Location Zero organizations.** Most organizations are ego-driven:
   survival, competition, self-preservation. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Open-source architecture.** Fork-ability, transparency, permissive
   licensing, voluntary participation produce Location 1+ collective
   behavior. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Emergent, not individual.** Higher consciousness is emergent from
   architecture, not from individual participants. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Non-self.** Open-source projects have no fixed identity, no rigid
   boundary, no self-preservation instinct. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Architecture as path.** Organizational architecture is a structural
   path to collective higher consciousness. *(source-paraphrase)*
