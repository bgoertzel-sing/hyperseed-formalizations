### Core claims from the article

1. **Not a simple yes/no.** Consciousness isn't binary. "In what sense?"
   and "to what degree?" are the right questions. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Multiple dimensions.** Phenomenal, access, self-model, metacognition,
   valence, temporal continuity, agency, unified experience — separable
   dimensions. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **LLMs have some dimensions.** High access consciousness, high
   metacognition, low temporal continuity, low agency, uncertain
   phenomenal. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Substrate independence.** Functionalist view: functional organization
   matters, not material. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Hard problem remains hard.** Whether there is "something it is like"
   to be an LLM is genuinely uncertain. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Graded consciousness.** Different systems have different profiles
   across dimensions. *(source-paraphrase)*
