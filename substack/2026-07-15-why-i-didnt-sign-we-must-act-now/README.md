## Summary

Explains why Goertzel didn't sign the "We Must Act Now" open letter about
AI and jobs, despite agreeing with its surface premises.

### Core argument

1. **Blandly agreeable surface, sharper agenda beneath.** The letter says
   AI is powerful, will reshape the economy, and we should act — all true
   on the surface. But the specific framing (steering AI to "complement
   humans") encodes the Acemoglu school's anti-AGI agenda: steering AI
   development away from autonomous AGI toward tools-only.

2. **Prediction impossibility.** Nobody can predict which jobs AGI will
   replace vs. complement, at what point, with what impact. The steam
   engine analogy: nobody in 1760 could predict what it would do to
   employment. Expert committees cannot figure this out in advance.

3. **Genre of deceptive open letters.** The article traces a genre:
   tobacco industry's "Frank Statement" (1954), Oregon Petition on
   climate (1998), Pause Giant AI Experiments (2023) — all blandly
   agreeable on the surface, all carrying sharper agendas underneath.

4. **Decentralized alternative.** Instead of expert-committee steering,
   make AI development as decentralized, participatory, and self-organizing
   as possible. Real complementarity between humans and AI emerges from
   bottom-up ferment, not top-down engineering.

5. **Relationship, not specification.** Human-AI complementarity is a
   relationship worked out in practice through billions of interactions,
   not a property written into a policy brief.

### Connection to other Goertzel work

- **Fable Farce / Benevolent Throttling:** Same ratchet mechanism — bland
  safety/economic concern laundering into control mechanisms.
- **Seven Flavors:** Humanity-evil flavor (eternal regime via steering).
- **Avoiding AGI Catastrophe:** Stewardship hinge — who gets to steer?
- **Sanders ban:** Same pattern of legislative intent vs structural effect.

## Hyperseed ontology interpretation

### Surface agreement masking directed disagreement

Agreement at different levels of the directed type:

- **0-cell agreement.** At the surface level (AI is powerful, economy will
  change, we should act), there is genuine agreement. These are 0-cells —
  point-level observations that nearly everyone shares.

- **1-cell disagreement.** At the directed level (which direction to act,
  what "complement" means, who decides), there is fundamental disagreement.
  These are 1-cells — directed paths from the shared observations to
  different conclusions.

- **Letter exploits 0-cell consensus to smuggle 1-cell agenda.** By
  framing the 1-cell content (Acemoglu steering) as if it follows
  inevitably from the 0-cell consensus (AI is powerful), the letter
  smuggles a specific directed path under cover of shared starting points.

### Centralized steering as frozen directed type

Expert-committee steering freezes development trajectories:

- **Frozen directed type.** A small expert committee decides the trajectory
  of AI development — which paths to explore, which to shut down. This
  freezes the directed type: the set of possible development paths is
  constrained by committee decision rather than organic exploration.

- **Same structure as lock-in.** This is the same frozen-directed-type
  pathology as the "eternal regime" flavor from Seven Flavors: a small
  group captures the development trajectory, preventing the multi-path
  exploration that healthy development requires.

- **Prediction impossibility = undecidable path selection.** If nobody
  can predict which jobs AGI will replace (steam engine analogy), then
  the committee's path selection is based on undecidable information.
  Freezing the directed type based on undecidable information is
  structurally guaranteed to be wrong.

### Decentralized development as parallel transport across many fibers

Many independent development paths running simultaneously:

- **Parallel transport.** Different architectures, value systems, cultures,
  economic models each transport the AI development fiber along different
  paths in the base space. The diversity of transport paths is the anti-
  scalar-collapse principle applied to AI governance.

- **Anti-scalar-collapse.** Collapsing the development trajectory to a
  single committee-approved path is scalar collapse: reducing a rich
  multi-dimensional development space to a single dimension controlled
  by a single authority.

- **Self-organizing complementarity.** Real human-AI complementarity
  emerges from bottom-up interaction, not top-down specification. It's
  a global section reconstructed from local data (a sheaf property),
  not imposed by fiat.

### Deceptive open letters as provenance manipulation

The genre of deceptive letters manipulates provenance cues:

- **Prestigious signatures as provenance cues.** Expert signatures provide
  a provenance cue that implies "this is expert-verified knowledge." But
  the signatures may not reflect genuine expert judgment on the specific
  directed claims — only agreement with the bland surface.

- **Same provenance theatre as KYC.** The signature-as-endorsement pattern
  is the same broken provenance as KYC: the cue exists but doesn't carry
  the information it claims to. The expert signed the 0-cell content but
  is being cited as endorsing the 1-cell content.

### Complementarity as emergent sheaf

Human-AI complementarity is a global section from local data:

- **Local interactions.** Billions of human-AI interactions in specific
  contexts produce local complementarity data: in this context, human does
  X and AI does Y, and the combination works.

- **Global section.** The overall pattern of human-AI complementarity is
  the global section reconstructed from all the local data — a sheaf
  property, not a designed property.

- **Cannot be specified top-down.** The sheaf condition (global section
  consistent with all local data) cannot be satisfied by top-down
  specification because the local data doesn't exist yet. You must
  generate the local data (through actual interactions) before you can
  reconstruct the global section.

### d-calculus connection (Hyperseed v2)

- **Surface-depth curvature.** The curvature between 0-cell (surface)
  and 1-cell (directed) content of the letter:

  ||F_∇(0-cell, 1-cell)|| = agenda smuggling severity

  High curvature: the 1-cell content (specific policy agenda) diverges
  sharply from what the 0-cell content (bland truisms) implies. Low
  curvature: the 1-cell content follows naturally from the 0-cell content.
  The article argues this curvature is high — the letter's specific
  agenda doesn't follow from its bland premises.

- **Development trajectory holonomy.** A loop through the steering cycle
  (committee convenes → restricts paths → observes outcomes → reconvenes)
  produces holonomy:

  Hol_γ(Γ_steering) = development diversity loss per steering cycle

  Increasing holonomy: each cycle reduces diversity more (path
  concentration, increasing homogeneity of AI development). The
  decentralized alternative targets zero holonomy: each cycle
  maintains or increases diversity.

- **Prediction uncertainty curvature.** The curvature of prediction
  confidence with respect to time horizon:

  ||F_∇(prediction, horizon)|| = prediction degradation rate

  High curvature: prediction confidence drops rapidly with horizon
  (the actual situation for AGI impact — nobody can predict even a
  few years out). This high curvature is the formal content of the
  steam engine analogy: the curvature is too high for committee
  steering to work.

- **Complementarity emergence curvature.** The curvature between
  top-down specification and bottom-up emergence of complementarity:

  ||F_∇(specified, emergent)|| = specification gap

  High curvature: specified complementarity diverges from emergent
  complementarity (the specification misses what actually works).
  Low curvature: specification tracks emergence (the specification
  captures what actually works). The article argues this curvature
  is high — you can't specify complementarity, you must let it emerge.

- **Provenance manipulation gradient.** The gradient of provenance
  reliability across the deceptive-letter genre:

  ∇_provenance = ∇(signature_authenticity × claim_endorsement ×
                     agenda_transparency)

  Steep gradient: some dimensions are reliable (signatures are real)
  while others are unreliable (claims aren't genuinely endorsed,
  agenda isn't transparent). The genre exploits the steep gradient:
  using reliable dimensions (real signatures) to imply reliability
  in unreliable dimensions (specific claims).
