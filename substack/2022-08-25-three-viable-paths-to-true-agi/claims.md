# Claim inventory — Three Viable Paths to True AGI

Source: Ben Goertzel, "Three Viable Paths to True AGI,"
Eurykosmotron, 2022-08-25.

## Epistemic-status key

- **source-paraphrase**: directly stated or closely implied in the article.
- **inferred**: not stated verbatim but follows from the article's logic.
- **hypothesis**: proposed by the article as a conjecture.
- **hyperseed-interpretation**: formalized within the Hyperseed ontology.

### Core claims from the article

1. **Hybrid neural-symbolic.** Combining neural and symbolic in integrated
   architectures (Hyperon). Neural for perception/generalization, symbolic
   for reasoning/planning. Integration = cognitive synergy. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Brain emulation.** Scanning and simulating brain structure at sufficient
   detail to reproduce cognition. Requires neuroscience, scanning tech, and
   computation advances. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Evolutionary/developmental.** Growing AGI through evolution, developmental
   learning, and embodied interaction. Discovers novel architectures.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Complementary paths.** The three paths inform each other: emulation reveals
   what works, evolution discovers novelty, hybrid provides interpretable
   testbeds. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Risk profiles differ.** Hybrid risks design errors; emulation risks unknown
   requirements; evolution risks unpredictability. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Scaling ≠ AGI.** Scaling LLMs is not a path to true AGI — qualitative
   architectural innovation is needed. *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **Resource misallocation.** Current funding overwhelmingly targets LLM scaling
   rather than the three viable paths. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Hyperseed-ontology claims

8. **Designed fiber (hybrid).** Fiber bundle explicitly composed from neural,
   symbolic, memory, action fiber types. Challenge: emergent properties from
   composition. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

9. **Copied fiber (emulation).** Fiber bundle copied from biological system.
   Challenge: determining essential vs. artifact fiber. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

10. **Evolved fiber (evolutionary).** Fiber bundle emerges through selection on
    variation. Challenge: slow, unpredictable, possibly incomprehensible.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

11. **Fiber cross-pollination.** Complementarity = cross-pollination between
    construction methods. Emulation reveals reference fiber; evolution discovers
    novel compositions; hybrid provides interpretable landscapes.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

12. **Construction curvature (d-calculus).** Each method produces characteristic
    curvature: designed = interface curvature, copied = biological curvature,
    evolved = unpredictable but functional curvature. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

13. **Fiber growth dynamics (d-calculus).** ∂F/∂t differs: designed = modular
    and controllable, copied = biological plasticity, evolved = generational.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

14. **Holonomy signatures (d-calculus).** Each method produces characteristic
    holonomy patterns — fingerprints of construction history.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

15. **Scaling curvature divergence (d-calculus).** Scaling increases fiber volume
    without changing curvature. AGI requires specific curvature patterns
    (cognitive synergy, self-modification holonomy) absent from scaled fixed
    architectures. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*
