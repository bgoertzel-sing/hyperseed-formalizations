# How Superhuman Superminds Will (Mostly) Transcend Suffering

**Source:** [bengoertzel.substack.com/p/how-superhuman-superminds-will-mostly](https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/how-superhuman-superminds-will-mostly)
**Date:** 2022-03-12

## Summary

Argues that posthuman superminds can substantially transcend suffering through engineered non-attachment, I-Thou intersubjective experience, and collective pain-pooling. Pain asymbolia demonstrates that pain sensation and pain suffering are separable. Non-attached minds experience pain without clinging, opening space for deep Second Person experience where collective joy-pain pooling yields net transcendence.

## Hyperseed Ontology Interpretation

the fiber of subjective suffering over the base of nociceptive signals has a trivializable connection under non-attachment; the d-calculus of I-Thou experience maps individual pain-fibers into a collective bundle where holonomy around the group yields net transcendence rather than net suffering
