## Summary

A systematic typology of AGI catastrophic risks, organized by two axes:
(a) source of the failure (humanity, the AGI, or the human–AGI supersystem)
and (b) mode of the failure (stupidity or evil, defined via open-ended
intelligence pathology). Key threads:

1. **Open-ended intelligence theory of evil.** Evil = pathology of the
   individuation/self-transcendence dialectic. Four modes: stagnation
   (too little self-transformation), grandiosity (too much), self-protection
   (too much individuation), self-destruction (too little individuation).

2. **"Good guys usually win" argument.** Cooperative strategies have
   structural advantages (information sharing, diverse perspectives,
   specialization) that tend to prevail given enough time and space.
   Evil strategies must capture the environment fast enough to prevent
   this dynamic from operating.

3. **Seven flavors organized as a 3×2+1 grid:**
   - (1) Humanity is stupid — deployment cascade, burning observability
   - (2) Humanity is evil — eternal regime, plutocratic runaway
   - (3) AGI is stupid — self-modification blunder, benign system error
   - (4) AGI is evil — instrumental convergence, convergent alien values
   - (5) AGI is beyond our ken — universal attractor, complexity threshold
   - (6) Emergent stupidity — flash crash, epistemic commons collapse
   - (7) Emergent evil — surveillance equilibrium, arms race as emergent
     grandiosity

4. **Arms race as cross-cutting attractor.** Not a single cell but spans
   multiple: human stupidity, human evil, potential AGI stupidity/evil,
   and emergent dynamics. Decentralization as structural defense.

5. **Decentralized AGI as mitigation.** Open, distributed development
   with many independent agents prevents the lock-in that evil scenarios
   require and enables the cooperative dynamics that give good outcomes
   their structural advantage.

### Connection to other Goertzel work

- **Avoiding AGI Catastrophe Pts 1-2:** The Seven Hinges framework

- **Tag, You're Not It / OpenBGI:** Agent-first societies as the

## Hyperseed ontology interpretation

### Evil modes as fiber-bundle deformations

Each evil mode maps to a specific deformation of the epistemic bundle:

- **Stagnation = trivial holonomy.** The system cannot be changed by
  experience; all loops in the directed type return to the same state.
  The fiber is static — no learning, no adaptation. Structurally, the
  connection is flat and the transport is trivial.

- **Grandiosity = uncontrolled holonomy.** The system changes faster than
  it can verify coherence. Transport around loops produces wildly
  different results depending on path — the connection has high curvature
  and the cocycle condition is violated (transition functions inconsistent).

- **Self-protection = rigid cocycle.** Transition functions become identity
  maps. The system refuses input from outside its own perspective — a
  closed epistemic cage. The fiber is well-defined but has degenerate
  transition functions that reject all external data.

- **Self-destruction = fiber collapse.** The fiber loses dimensionality.
  The system's identity dissolves — it can no longer maintain a distinct
  perspective. In the limit, the fiber is zero-dimensional (no internal
  state at all).

### Seven flavors as catastrophe landscape

The seven flavors populate a catastrophe landscape in the
(source × mode) parameter space:

- **Deployment cascade = cocycle race condition.** Multiple actors pushing
  sections of the bundle without coordinating transition functions.
  The cocycle condition fails at the interfaces between competing
  deployments.

- **Burning observability = provenance destruction.** Same anti-E_π
  structure as the Sanders ban's selection effect. Provenance pathways
  are destroyed for cost savings, removing the inspection capability
  needed at critical thresholds.

- **Lock-in (eternal regime, plutocratic runaway) = frozen directed type.**
  Capturing the environment prevents new cells from being generated in
  the directed type. The system reaches a fixed point that isn't an
  attractor of healthy dynamics but a trap enforced by power.

- **Emergent stupidity = cognitive synergy failure.** The supersystem's
  fiber bundle has nontrivial holonomy in each component but the components
  don't cohere — failed descent data. Each subsystem works locally but
  global coordination fails.

- **Arms race = emergent grandiosity.** Collective self-transformation
  (competition-driven capability escalation) outruns integration capacity.
  The directed type grows new cells faster than the cocycle can verify
  them — uncontrolled holonomy at the system level.

- **Universal attractor / beyond our ken = undecidable fiber property.**
  There may be fiber properties of superintelligent systems that are
  computationally undecidable from our current position — we literally
  cannot determine whether the attractor is benign.

### Good-guys advantage as cooperative fiber topology

The structural advantage of cooperation is a topological property:

- **Cooperative topology.** Multiple agents with diverse fibers connected
  by non-trivial transition functions. Information flows through the
  bundle; diverse perspectives correct each other's local errors. The
  fiber bundle has rich topology enabling error correction.

- **Evil topology.** Requires collapsing the bundle to a trivial topology
  (single fiber, no diversity, no independent correction). This is
  structurally fragile — any perturbation can break the single-fiber
  assumption. Evil must maintain the collapse actively, which costs energy.

- **Time and space = sufficient cells.** The cooperative advantage needs
  enough cells in the directed type (enough time for the dynamics to
  play out, enough space for diverse agents to coexist). Evil wins when
  it can prevent new cells from forming — lock-in, suppression, capture.

### d-calculus connection (Hyperseed v2)

- **Individuation-transcendence curvature.** The curvature of the
  (ι, σ) parameter space:

  ||F_∇(ι, σ)|| = pathology proximity

  High curvature near the boundary of H (homeostatic region): small
  changes flip between healthy and pathological. Low curvature in the
  interior: robust health. The d-calculus measures how close a system
  is to each evil mode by computing curvature in the relevant direction.

- **Stagnation holonomy (d-calculus).** Transport around any loop
  produces trivial holonomy:

  Hol_γ(Γ_stagnation) ≈ Id for all γ

  The system is frozen. The d-calculus curvature is zero but this is
  pathological — it indicates a flat connection (no learning), not a
  well-behaved one.

- **Grandiosity curvature (d-calculus).** Curvature is unbounded:

  ||F_∇_grandiosity|| → ∞

  The connection has divergent curvature — the cocycle condition is
  violated, transition functions are inconsistent. The d-calculus
  detects this as curvature blow-up: the fiber bundle is
  mathematically ill-defined in the limit.

- **Lock-in curvature (d-calculus).** The curvature of the directed
  type under lock-in:

  ||F_∇(frozen_type)|| = 0 (artificially maintained)

  Zero curvature maintained by external force (power), not by genuine
  equilibrium. The d-calculus can distinguish between genuine zero
  curvature (healthy equilibrium) and maintained zero curvature
  (frozen pathology) by checking whether the connection is covariantly
  constant (genuine) or rigidly enforced (artificial).

- **Arms race holonomy (d-calculus).** A loop through the competitive
  cycle (develop → deploy → observe competitor → escalate) produces
  holonomy:

  Hol_γ(Γ_arms_race) = capability escalation per competitive cycle

  Increasing holonomy: the arms race accelerates (each cycle produces
  more escalation than the last — positive feedback). Decreasing
  holonomy: the arms race decelerates (cooperative dynamics gaining
  ground). The d-calculus holonomy tracks which dynamic dominates.

- **Cooperative topology gradient.** The gradient from fragile to
  robust cooperative topology:

  ∇_cooperation = ∇(fiber_diversity × transition_richness ×
                     error_correction_capacity × cell_generation_rate)

  Steep gradient: system is near a tipping point between cooperation
  and collapse. Flat gradient: robustly cooperative. The design goal
  is to place the system in a flat-gradient region — robustly
  cooperative, hard to tip.

- **Catastrophe landscape curvature tensor.** The full curvature tensor
  over the (source × mode) parameter space:

  F_∇_catastrophe ∈ Ω²(Source × Mode)

  Each flavor is a region of this tensor with characteristic curvature.
  The overall safety of the system is determined by which regions the
  current configuration is near. Low curvature everywhere: system is
  far from all catastrophes. High curvature in any direction: system is
  near that flavor of catastrophe.
