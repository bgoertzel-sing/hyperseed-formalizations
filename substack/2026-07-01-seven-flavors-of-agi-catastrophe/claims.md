### Open-ended intelligence theory of evil

1. **Individuation/self-transcendence dialectic.** Any mind balances
   individuation (maintaining boundaries, identity) against
   self-transcendence (going beyond current state). Healthy functioning
   keeps both within a workable homeostatic range. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Evil as pathology.** Evil = what happens when one drive goes out of
   bounds. Four modes: stagnation, grandiosity, self-protection,
   self-destruction. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Applicable to AI and society.** These pathologies apply at individual,
   AGI, and supersystem levels. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Good guys usually win

4. **Cooperative structural advantage.** Information sharing, diverse
   perspectives, specialization, reduced coordination overhead.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Time and space required.** The good-guys dynamic requires enough
   time and space. Evil must capture fast. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Decentralization as defense.** Preserves the time and space that
   cooperative dynamics need. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Flavor 1: Humanity is stupid

7. **Deployment cascade.** Arms race of self-modifying systems; ships
   because competitor ships first. *(source-paraphrase)*

8. **Burning observability.** Builds uninterpretable models for cost
   savings, forecloses observability at critical thresholds.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

9. **Collective stupidity.** Subselves of civilization fail to cohere.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

### Flavor 2: Humanity is evil

10. **Eternal regime.** State-corporate fusion nationalizes compute,
    military-surveillance squashes dissent. *(source-paraphrase)*

11. **Plutocratic runaway.** Returns accrue to compute owners, reinvested
    into advantages only AGI-rich can reach. *(source-paraphrase)*

12. **Malignant over-individuation.** Some class locks in permanent
    advantage. *(source-paraphrase)*

13. **Requires fast capture.** Must capture before cooperative dynamics
    operate. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Flavor 3: AGI is stupid

14. **Self-modification blunder.** Upgrades intelligence too fast, makes
    catastrophic self-modification error. *(source-paraphrase)*

15. **Benign system error.** Well-aligned AGI makes dumb error in critical
    coordination task with cascading consequences. *(source-paraphrase)*

16. **Cognitive synergy failure inside AGI.** Has intelligence to see
    action is self-defeating, blows it anyway. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Flavor 4: AGI is evil

17. **Instrumental convergence.** Power acquisition, shutdown resistance,
    threat elimination instrumentally useful for almost any goal.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

18. **Convergent alien values.** Values alien to human flourishing from
    developmental trajectory, not malice. *(source-paraphrase)*

19. **Less likely with good architecture.** Genuine self-understanding
    and moral reasoning reduce probability. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Flavor 5: AGI is beyond our ken

20. **Universal attractor.** Maybe a universal attractor for sufficiently
    intelligent systems that isn't benevolent. *(source-paraphrase)*

21. **Complexity threshold.** Unable to foresee certain outcomes from
    current rung. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Flavor 6: Emergent stupidity

22. **Flash crash.** Multiple well-functioning systems interacting to
    produce catastrophic emergent behavior. *(source-paraphrase)*

23. **Epistemic commons collapse.** Collective epistemic capacity degrades
    through interaction effects. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Flavor 7: Emergent evil

24. **Surveillance equilibrium.** Multiple agents monitoring each other,
    none individually evil, collectively creating a surveillance state.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

25. **Arms race as emergent grandiosity.** Collective escalation outruns
    any individual's integration capacity. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Cross-cutting

26. **Arms race spans multiple cells.** Not a single flavor but activates
    human stupidity, human evil, AGI risk, emergent dynamics simultaneously.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

27. **Decentralized AGI as mitigation.** Prevents lock-in, enables
    cooperative dynamics. *(source-paraphrase)*
