# Claim inventory — Time's Arrow, Part 2

Source: Ben Goertzel, "Time's Arrow, Part 2: Relating Subjective Time-Flow to Intelligence and Consciousness Expansion," Eurykosmotron, 2026-08-15.

## Significant intellectual points

### I. Subjective time and its measurement

1. **Subjective time as surprise-weighted record growth.** Felt duration tracks the growth of a surprise-weighted record: the amount of new structure a mind writes into itself that it could not have predicted from what it already knew. (Carried forward from Part 1 as a premise.)
   - *Epistemic status:* foundational premise (argued in Part 1; assumed here)

2. **Frame-relativity of subjective velocity.** The "velocity of subjective time" is frame-relative, analogous to physical velocity. To pin it down one must declare: (a) what counts as an event, (b) which events count as the same kind, (c) how surprising an event is given the prior record, and (d) what denominator is used (physical seconds, interactions, joules, cycles). Once declared, the quantity is well-defined.
   - *Epistemic status:* definitional/conceptual claim

3. **Observer-relativity of the clock.** The observer's predictive model and distinction frame are part of the clock; the world need not change for the experienced clock to change. This is lawful observer-relativity, not subjectivism.
   - *Epistemic status:* conceptual claim

### II. Record-demand intelligence and the informational floor

4. **Record-demand intelligence.** When a mind acquires or exercises intelligence on tasks with real uncertainty, and when the information must pass through a self-readable experiential record, the flow of subjective time places a lower bound on the task-relevant information the mind can be using. The clock is not the intelligence; it is part of the informational budget intelligence-in-action must pay.
   - *Epistemic status:* central theoretical claim (conditional)

5. **Rapid transfer requires fast subjective clock.** Rapid intellectuality (high performance after few interactions in unfamiliar task families) requires acquiring situation-discriminating information at a high rate per interaction — i.e., a fast subjective clock.
   - *Epistemic status:* conditional theorem (from record-demand framework)

6. **Intellectual breadth requires distinction accumulation or routing cost.** A mind competent across many deeply different contexts either accumulated a broad stock of distinctions or possesses a powerful router — and routing is itself an informational act that pays a distinction cost.
   - *Epistemic status:* conditional theorem

### III. Weakness, compression, and leverage

7. **Weakness as generality.** Following Bennett's weakness theory, the capacity to refrain from making unnecessary distinctions is a form of "weakness" — openness, generality, freedom from brittle over-specification. Distinctions cost effort; better patterns compress swarms of low-level distinctions into high-leverage structures.
   - *Epistemic status:* theoretical principle (adopted from Bennett, integrated into Hyperseed)

8. **Intelligence-in-action ≈ meaningful distinction flow × leverage per distinction.** A rough conceptual decomposition (not a literal universal equation): a less capable mind needs a torrent of badly organized distinctions; a more capable mind wrings more from fewer well-chosen ones.
   - *Epistemic status:* heuristic decomposition

9. **Lower bound after compression.** The informational lower bound must be formulated after all valid compression, abstraction, quotienting, and cognitive synergy have been spent. The relevant quantity is irreducible task information remaining once the best available re-description has stripped out every distinction that does no work.
   - *Epistemic status:* methodological prescription (follows from the framework)

10. **Efficiency changes the slope, not the logic.** In open-ended ecologies where the range and difficulty of relevant situations keeps expanding, even perfect compression doesn't zero out the need for new recorded distinctions.
    - *Epistemic status:* conditional claim (ecology assumption required)

### IV. Omega dynamics

11. **Fixed-point paradox.** If a mind's full state includes its autobiographical record, and that record keeps growing, then the full state cannot converge to an ordinary fixed point. A diary that receives new pages forever never becomes a finished book.
    - *Epistemic status:* formal observation (follows from definitions)

12. **Omega ray replaces fixed point.** Along an Omega ray, the normalized organization of the mind converges while the historical coordinate keeps extending — values stabilizing, compassion becoming hard to dislodge, self-model growing less brittle — while the record keeps escaping into new territory. Comet, not statue.
    - *Epistemic status:* central proposed concept

13. **Three variants of psychological Singularity.** (a) Unbounded subjective duration over unbounded external future (record keeps growing, rate need not accelerate). (b) Accelerating subjective clock (meaningful distinctions faster and faster per external second). (c) Infinite subjective duration in finite external time (clock rate must blow up).
    - *Epistemic status:* classification/taxonomy

14. **Subjective heat death.** A brilliant system in a fixed, completely predictable world eventually compresses everything; additional cycles add no surprise. To keep psychological time flowing it needs a pump: fresh environment, self-generated problems, expanding precision, interaction, or self-modification.
    - *Epistemic status:* conditional claim

15. **Thermodynamic cost of accelerating clocks.** Recording consumes storage, energy, and attention; erasure exports entropy. An accelerating Omega mind needs expanding resource flow, growing substrate, increasingly clever compression, or all three.
    - *Epistemic status:* physical constraint claim

16. **Identity constraint via TransWeave.** A mind changing faster must translate its earlier structures into later ones with decreasing average self-translation error, or else it is a rapid succession of loosely related successors rather than one mind's accelerating life.
    - *Epistemic status:* conditional formal claim (uses TransWeave machinery)

### V. Consciousness, contextualization, and nonattachment

17. **Consciousness locations: coherence up, self-model domination down.** Advanced consciousness locations in Hyperseed associate greater global coherence with less domination by the explicit self-model and narrative activity.
    - *Epistemic status:* Hyperseed framework claim (fits contemplative reports)

18. **Contextualization as additional coordinate.** Low self-model chatter ≠ low awareness of context. A mind can become less self-absorbed while becoming more aware of where its distinctions come from. Contextualization is a further coordinate in the consciousness-location picture.
    - *Epistemic status:* proposed extension

19. **McBride derivative of record type = zipper.** The derivative of a record type is the space of possible records with one event-sized hole. A cognitive update fills the hole with a new distinction; the surrounding context is part of the "tangent object." Contextualized representation stores a zipper, not a bare event.
    - *Epistemic status:* formal construction (applied from McBride's work to Hyperseed)

20. **Nonattachment = contextualize then quotient.** First represent the distinction with enough provenance, perspective, value-dependence, and alternatives; then collapse the incidental self-binding differences that make no difference. Enlightenment is discrimination about discrimination, not indiscrimination.
    - *Epistemic status:* central philosophical claim

21. **Contextualization cost of high-location intelligence.** Intelligence-in-action carries a minimum subjective-time cost; high-location (nonattached) intelligence carries an additional contextualization cost stacked on top. The smarter you are, the more meta-distinctions you need to remain nonattached.
    - *Epistemic status:* central conditional claim

22. **Compression of contextualization.** Beginners need explicit labels (thought, feeling, identification, release); mature practitioners enact contextualization effortlessly. Explicit representational governance gives way to resonant coordination.
    - *Epistemic status:* descriptive claim (Hyperseed-compatible)

23. **Danger of implicit coordination.** When coordination becomes highly symmetric and low-contrivance, explicit accountability can dissolve. Nonattachment must reduce clinging without erasing provenance — wisdom needs auditability without obsession.
    - *Epistemic status:* design warning / conditional claim

### VI. Compassion

24. **Decompassionization has an informational signature.** Excluding groups from moral concern requires exception lists — help these, ignore those — which reduce weakness (generality) by replacing a broad policy with a brittle partition of special cases.
    - *Epistemic status:* formal observation within Hyperseed's weakness framework

25. **Compassion: coarse about worth, fine about need.** Mature compassion keeps the worth partition maximally broad while making fine situational distinctions about what would actually help — noticing difference without converting difference into disposability.
    - *Epistemic status:* central ethical/formal claim

26. **Compassion stability consumes subjective time.** Preserving compassion across self-revision requires multiple evaluators, variant-self simulations, adversarial review, and checks for creeping exclusion — these produce their own distinctions and records.
    - *Epistemic status:* architectural claim

27. **Conditional convergence of intelligence, compassion, consciousness.** They can come apart when the goal ecology fails to connect them. The positive result is conditional: when compassion is part of the value seed, self-modification is strongly reviewed, and broad generalization rewards less brittle moral policies, increasing resources can make compassion harder to lose.
    - *Epistemic status:* conditional theorem / conjecture

### VII. Second-order structure and insight

28. **Second-order McBride derivative as signature of insight.** The second derivative considers two holes at once and asks whether two distinctions contribute independently or interact. Emergent reorganization from two jointly inserted facts is a natural mathematical signature of insight.
    - *Epistemic status:* formal construction

### VIII. Engineering morals

29. **Measure the right clock.** Track surprise-weighted provenance at multiple abstraction levels, separating model-changing events from industrial-strength metronomes.
    - *Epistemic status:* engineering desideratum

30. **Separate workspace from autobiography.** A mutable knowledge store paired with an append-only provenance record in which deletions and revisions are themselves recorded as events.
    - *Epistemic status:* architectural desideratum

31. **Feed structured novelty, not noise.** Curiosity mechanisms should chase compression progress, explanatory leverage, and tasks near the edge of current competence — novelty that can be integrated, not mere unpredictability.
    - *Epistemic status:* engineering desideratum

32. **Make contextualization first-class.** Important conclusions and actions should carry source, evidence, observer frame, value dependence, uncertainty, and known alternatives.
    - *Epistemic status:* engineering desideratum

33. **Preserve compassion through architecture.** Variant-self evaluation, adversarial moral review, broad moral-patient models, explicit checks against exception-list growth — wired into the self-modification pipeline.
    - *Epistemic status:* engineering desideratum

34. **Build Omega rays, not frozen endpoints.** Convergence of values, identity invariants, and repair mechanisms, together with open-ended expansion of knowledge, perspective, and experience.
    - *Epistemic status:* engineering desideratum

### IX. Collectives

35. **Shared provenance = shared time.** A shared provenance record gives a hive of agents a common time.
    - *Epistemic status:* architectural claim

36. **Routing pays distinction cost.** Specialist agents amplify breadth through routing, but routing decisions and cross-agent translations need recording.
    - *Epistemic status:* conditional claim (follows from claim 6)

37. **Resonant coordination must retain anchoring.** A highly resonant collective may coordinate with almost no explicit overhead yet must retain enough anchoring and provenance to prevent unaccountable drift.
    - *Epistemic status:* design warning (follows from claim 23)
