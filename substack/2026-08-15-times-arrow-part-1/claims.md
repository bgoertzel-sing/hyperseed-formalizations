## Core thesis

1. **Arrow as record growth.** An arrow of time is the growth of recorded
   distinctions — the universe, or some piece of it, keeping records of its own
   self-differentiation. The several classic arrows (thermodynamic, radiative,
   quantum, gravitational, psychological) are instances of one mathematical
   structure. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Graphtropy as foundation.** Graphtropy — a generalization of entropy on
   distinction graphs, where vertices are possible observations and edges link
   distinguishable pairs — is the right formal substrate for time's arrow
   because it is built from the observer's side from the start.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Observer-relativity of arrows.** Arrows are relative to an observer's
   distinction graph (way of looking), analogous to how velocities are relative
   to a reference frame, without implying that the arrow is illusory.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

## Three growth strengths

4. **On-average growth.** A quantity can grow on average while fluctuating
   downward arbitrarily often. Physical observables typically exhibit this kind
   of growth. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Exponential-rate growth.** A quantity can grow at a long-run exponential
   rate with unlimited short-run reversals. Optical amplifiers and driven media
   exhibit this. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Monotone (strict) growth.** A structure can grow monotonically along
   every path, never shrinking. Physical observables essentially never do this;
   only records can. *(source-paraphrase)*

7. **Records as strict arrows.** The strict arrow of time, wherever it
   appears, is the growth of a record: the accumulated history of thresholds
   passed, events logged, distinctions registered. You cannot un-ring a bell
   that has been written down. *(source-paraphrase)*

## Thermodynamic arrow

8. **Entropy production as signed likelihood ratio.** The entropy production of
   a process is the logarithm of how much more probable the forward movie is
   than the time-reversed movie: $\sigma = \log(P_{\mathrm{fwd}}/P_{\mathrm{rev}})$.
   Its sign is the orientation of the arrow; its magnitude is the strength.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

9. **Second law as on-average arrow.** Fluctuation theorems (Jarzynski–Crooks
   family) show that entropy production is an on-average and long-run-rate
   arrow; the strict arrow is whatever record the entropy-producing process
   writes. *(source-paraphrase)*

10. **Two movements of the second law.** (a) Distribution-level mixing: distinctions
    between differently prepared systems die away (cream into coffee, distinction
    graph thinning). (b) Record-level museum: the same processes write durable
    distinctions elsewhere (broken shells, scorch marks, geological strata) —
    the world is largely a museum of past thermodynamic events.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

11. **Landauer ledger.** Record-writing is itself a thermodynamic process, paid
    for in the currency it documents. Landauer's erasure bound is one line of a
    longer exact accounting (the "ledger theorem"). *(source-paraphrase)*

12. **Coarse-graining as frame-relativity.** In the distinction-record approach,
    coarse-graining is the explicit frame-relativity of the formalism — the
    observer's distinction graph captures the character of coarseness more
    cleanly than a partition into cells. *(source-paraphrase)*

13. **Poincaré recurrence and arrows.** Recurrent systems (e.g., Kac's ring
    model) can relax at the ensemble level while being exactly periodic; this
    constrains but does not eliminate arrows on finite recurrent systems.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

## Radiative arrow

14. **Retardation asymmetry.** Maxwell's equations admit retarded (outward) and
    advanced (inward) solutions; nature uses only retarded waves. This radiative
    arrow has long been debated as independent from or derivable from the
    thermodynamic arrow. *(source-paraphrase)*

15. **Time-varying media arrow.** Waves in time-varying media (materials whose
    optical properties are modulated) obey equations admitting only forward-in-time
    solutions, producing correlated photon pairs from the vacuum.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

16. **Escape into unboundedness.** What makes time-varying media tick is escape
    into unboundedness: the state of each mode pair lives on a curved unbounded
    surface, the drive pushes it into fresh territory, and there is always
    something new to record. *(source-paraphrase)*

17. **Photon pairs as written history.** The created photon pairs, once
    decohered, are the written history — the canonical arrow is literally a
    causal past, the accumulating set of excitation thresholds crossed,
    inscribed in light. *(source-paraphrase)*

18. **Five conditions skeleton.** Cosmological particle creation during inflation
    and the dynamical Casimir effect share the same skeleton under five precise
    conditions, none mentioning relativity. *(source-paraphrase)*

19. **Lossless medium is time-symmetric.** The idealized lossless time-varying
    medium is provably time-symmetric; genuine law-level arrows must be inherited
    from dissipation. *(source-paraphrase)*

## Quantum arrow

20. **Decoherence as record-writing.** A measurement outcome is a distinction;
    decoherence is the act of recording it; quantum Darwinism says reality is
    information redundantly imprinted in environmental fragments.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

21. **Genericity insufficiency.** The celebrated genericity theorems show
    widely-copied information is classical-like but do not show that copying
    accumulates — a generic environment can scramble and retract copies.
    Monotone growth of quantum records requires a structural hypothesis.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

22. **Freshness hypothesis.** Each environmental fragment arrives uncorrelated,
    interacts once, and departs forever, never returning to un-tell what it was
    told. This is the structural hypothesis needed for monotone quantum record
    growth. *(hypothesis)*

23. **Huygens escape principle.** In three spatial dimensions, Huygens'
    principle ensures light propagates sharply on its cone — radiated fragments
    make exactly one pass and escape into the expanding dark. This proves the
    freshness hypothesis for photonic environmental fragments.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

24. **Radiative-quantum arrow unification.** The forward-onlyness of radiation
    and the once-and-forever character of quantum measurement are two faces of
    the same escape — things leaving and never coming back — welding the
    radiative and quantum arrows at the level of mechanism.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

25. **Interpretation neutrality.** The construction speaks of records and channels;
    Everettians may read branches, pragmatists may read laboratory outcomes;
    the mathematics is indifferent. *(source-paraphrase)*

## Psychological arrow

26. **Neural states recur; spikes are events.** Brain states recur (firing rates
    cycle), so no honest arrow lives at the level of neural state. But spikes
    are events, each happening once, and the growing web of spikes-causing-spikes
    is the accumulating causal record. *(source-paraphrase)*

27. **Surprise-weighted record growth.** Each recorded event is valued by its
    surprise (unpredictability given everything recorded so far), making the
    growing record a compressed description and the internal clock rate the
    growth rate of that description. *(source-paraphrase)*

28. **Felt duration as description length.** Boredom = low surprise = slow clock;
    novel experiences = high surprise = fast clock; oddball effect follows as
    near-definitional; accelerating years of middle age = declining marginal
    surprise. *(source-paraphrase)*

29. **Spiking network simulations.** Izhikevich-style spiking neural network
    simulations show the predicted rise-and-fall of internal clock rate from
    order toward deep chaos; hypersynchronized (seizure-like) states register
    as clock-stoppage. *(source-paraphrase)*

30. **B-series and A-series.** McTaggart's B-series (tenseless web of events
    ordered by earlier-than) is the written record; the A-series (tensed
    past-now-future) is the writing frontier. The feeling of passage is what it
    is like to be a record in the process of being written.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

31. **Specious present.** The experienced now corresponds to the integration
    window over which spike events get bound into single recorded tokens.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

32. **Causal fork asymmetry.** Under explicit hypotheses, a record can carry
    information about its past and cannot carry information about its future —
    Reichenbach's causal fork — so the future feels open because nothing in
    your head is a memory of it. *(source-paraphrase)*

## Origins and alignment

33. **Janus point.** For a gravitating Newtonian system that never collapses,
    the moment of inertia is a convex function of time with a unique minimum
    (the Janus point). Each side expands, clusters, complexifies, and writes
    records. Observers on either side call "future" the direction away from the
    minimum. *(source-paraphrase)*

34. **No past hypothesis needed.** The low-entropy "beginning" is the waist of
    an hourglass, not an inexplicable initial edge — the direction of the arrow
    is a theorem, not a posit. *(source-paraphrase)*

35. **Arrow inheritance.** When a far-from-equilibrium source drives many
    subsystems, each subsystem's asymmetry is a shadow of the source's: the
    signed entropy-production quantity is passed down the chain of influence,
    orientation preserved, strength diminished at each generation (data-processing
    tax). Agreement of arrows = common descent from the same pump.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

36. **Freshness from escape.** The freshness conditions needed for the
    inheritance theorem can be derived from escape (Huygens' principle) rather
    than assumed, collapsing several suspicious assumptions into one
    mathematical premise: a mild condition on correlations at the Janus waist.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

## Records as correlations

37. **Records are correlations.** A record is a stabilized correlation between
    a register and the world — mutual information the dynamics protects.
    Distinctions are recorded in distinctions; the medium and message are the
    same kind of thing at every level — no regress of what-records-the-record.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

38. **Cosmological master copy.** The master copy of the universe's record is
    the outgoing radiation field receding at c; unerasability = unreadability.
    Memories, strata, and archives are local transcripts. The CMB is about the
    closest readable page. *(source-paraphrase)*

39. **Recordhood is reader-relative.** A pattern is a record with respect to
    some decoding dynamics, some actual or possible interpreter. This is
    continuous with the frame-relativity of the whole framework.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

## Application to AI (Hyperon)

40. **AtomSpace as native causal web.** Every Hyperon inference step creates
    knowledge atoms with derivation history; the causal web of events exists
    natively as a queryable data structure. *(source-paraphrase)*

41. **Append-only trail as strict arrow.** A rewritable knowledge store carries
    only an on-average arrow; a strict arrow needs an append-only trail (digital
    analogue of the escaping photon = consensus finality on a distributed
    ledger). *(source-paraphrase)*

42. **Surprise-weighted knowledge flux as internal clock.** The internal clock
    is the surprise-weighted flux of new knowledge — how unpredictable a new
    atom is given everything known. An AI in a hot loop re-deriving known
    conclusions experiences nothing; genuine novelty = lived time.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

43. **Curiosity as clock maintenance.** As a world-model matures, routine
    input compresses toward zero surprise and the inner clock decays toward
    stasis. Curiosity (the drive toward what resists compression) is clock
    maintenance. *(inferred)*

44. **Subjective heat death.** A system closed on its own outputs faces
    subjective heat death. Escapes: open the loop to a fresh world, or become
    your own pump via self-modification. *(source-paraphrase)*

45. **Self-modification and identity.** Self-modification preserving the
    append-only record is coherent self-transcendence; self-modification
    rewriting the record ends one mind's time and starts another's.
    *(source-paraphrase)*

46. **Shared record = shared time.** In multi-agent systems, a shared record
    is shared time; sub-agents on private mutable stores are opposite-arrow
    domains; merging them is an annihilation zone. *(source-paraphrase)*

47. **Engineering surface.** In Hyperon, the reader that makes the system's
    history a record for itself is a designed component; the specious present
    width is a parameter; temporal experience correlates are an engineering
    surface. *(source-paraphrase)*
