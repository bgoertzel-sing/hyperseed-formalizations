\section{Informal Hyperseed reading}

The article proposes that time's arrow — across thermodynamics, radiation,
quantum measurement, gravitation, and psychology — is \emph{one} phenomenon:
the growth of recorded distinctions.  ``Distinction'' is formalized via
graphtropy (an observer-relative generalization of entropy on graphs);
``growth'' is stratified into three strengths; ``record'' is a dynamically
stable correlation whose monotone accumulation constitutes the strict arrow.
The framework unifies the classic arrows by showing each is either a
fluctuating quantity being recorded (on-average arrow) or the record itself
(strict arrow), with arrows inherited down causal chains from a common
far-from-equilibrium pump whose own orientation derives from a Janus-point
gravitational minimum.  Application to Hyperon AI makes the causal web
native, the strict arrow an append-only ledger, and subjective time the
surprise-weighted knowledge flux.

The Hyperseed relevance is deep: graphtropy, observer-relativity, weakness
orderings, and the quantale of evidence/logic/energy all appear as core
formal ingredients.

\section{Core formal objects}

%% ---------- Distinction graph ----------
\begin{definition}[Distinction graph]\label{def:dg}
Let $\Omega$ be a set of possible observations and let $\cO$ be an observer
(a finite-resolution measurement apparatus).  The \emph{distinction graph}
of $\cO$ on $\Omega$ is
\[
  \cG_\cO = (\Omega,\; E_\cO), \qquad
  E_\cO = \bigl\{\{x,y\} \subseteq \Omega : \cO \text{ can distinguish } x
  \text{ from } y \bigr\}.
\]
Edges encode distinguishability at the observer's actual resolution.
The complement graph encodes indistinguishability (the coarse-graining).
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Graphtropy''; claims 2, 3, 12.}
\end{definition}

%% ---------- Graphtropy ----------
\begin{definition}[Graphtropy]\label{def:graphtropy}
Given a distinction graph $\cG_\cO = (\Omega, E_\cO)$ and a probability
distribution $p$ on $\Omega$, the \emph{graphtropy} is a family of
entropy-like functionals
\[
  \Grp_\alpha(\cG_\cO, p) \;=\; F_\alpha\!\bigl(p,\; E_\cO\bigr)
\]
parameterized by a resolution or order index $\alpha$, generalizing
Shannon/R\'enyi entropies by weighting contributions according to the
observer's distinguishability structure rather than a partition into cells.
When $\cG_\cO$ is a complete graph, graphtropy reduces to standard entropy;
when $E_\cO = \varnothing$, no distinctions exist and graphtropy is zero.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Graphtropy''; Goertzel (2019),
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.00741}{arXiv:1902.00741}; claims 2, 12.}
\end{definition}

%% ---------- Recorded distinction ----------
\begin{definition}[Recorded distinction]\label{def:record}
Let $\cS$ be a physical system evolving in time and let $\cR \subseteq \cS$
be a register subsystem.  A \emph{recorded distinction} at time $t$ is a
stable correlation between $\cR$ and the rest of $\cS$:
\[
  \MI\!\bigl(\cR_t ;\; \cS \setminus \cR_t\bigr) > 0,
\]
where $\MI$ is mutual information, and the correlation persists for times
$s > t$ under the system's dynamics (dynamical stability).  A \emph{record}
is a set of recorded distinctions
$\mathbf{R}(t) = \bigl\{r_1, r_2, \ldots, r_{|\mathbf{R}(t)|}\bigr\}$
accumulated up to time $t$.  Records are correlations — distinctions
recorded in distinctions — with no infinite regress, since each layer of
inscription is more correlation structure.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Records'', \S``Correlations''; claims 7, 37.}
\end{definition}

%% ---------- Three growth strengths ----------
\begin{definition}[Three growth strengths]\label{def:growth}
Let $Q(t)$ be a real-valued quantity or $|\mathbf{R}(t)|$ the cardinality
of a record, indexed by time $t$.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{On-average growth.}
    $\langle Q(t_2) - Q(t_1) \rangle \geq 0$ for $t_2 > t_1$, with downward
    fluctuations permitted on individual trajectories.
  \item \textbf{Exponential-rate growth.}
    $\liminf_{t \to \infty} \frac{1}{t}\log Q(t) > 0$, with unlimited
    short-run reversals.
  \item \textbf{Monotone (strict) growth.}
    $Q(t_2) \geq Q(t_1)$ for all $t_2 > t_1$ along every individual path,
    i.e., $Q$ is non-decreasing almost surely.
\end{enumerate}
Physical observables typically exhibit strengths 1 or 2.  The article argues
that only records can exhibit strength 3.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Three growth strengths''; claims 4--7.}
\end{definition}

%% ---------- Arrow of time ----------
\begin{definition}[Arrow of time]\label{def:arrow}
An \emph{arrow of time} on a system $\cS$ relative to observer $\cO$ is a
functional $\Phi_\cO(t)$ that grows in one of the three senses of
Definition~\ref{def:growth}.  A \emph{strict arrow} is one exhibiting
monotone growth; a \emph{fluctuating arrow} is one exhibiting on-average
or exponential-rate growth only.  The central thesis: every strict arrow is
the growth of a record $\mathbf{R}(t)$, and every fluctuating arrow is a
quantity being recorded.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Core idea''; claim 1.}
\end{definition}

%% ---------- Entropy production ----------
\begin{definition}[Entropy production as signed likelihood ratio]\label{def:ep}
For a stochastic process with forward path measure $P_{\mathrm{fwd}}$ and
time-reversed path measure $P_{\mathrm{rev}}$, the \emph{entropy production}
along a trajectory $\omega$ is
\[
  \sigma(\omega) \;=\; \log \frac{P_{\mathrm{fwd}}(\omega)}{P_{\mathrm{rev}}(\tilde{\omega})},
\]
where $\tilde{\omega}$ is the time-reversed trajectory.  The \emph{sign} of
$\sigma$ is the orientation of the arrow; the \emph{magnitude} is the
strength.  Treating $\sigma$ rather than entropy $S$ as the fundamental
carrier is what allows arrows to be inherited down chains of influence.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Entropy production''; claim 8.}
\end{definition}

%% ---------- Freshness hypothesis ----------
\begin{definition}[Freshness hypothesis]\label{def:freshness}
An environment $\cE$ of a quantum system $\cS$ satisfies \emph{freshness}
when each environmental fragment $e_i \in \cE$:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item arrives at $\cS$ uncorrelated with prior fragments,
  \item interacts with $\cS$ exactly once, and
  \item departs forever, never returning to reverse its informational imprint.
\end{enumerate}
Under freshness, the quantum record of decoherence events grows monotonically
(strict arrow).  Without freshness, environments can scramble and retract
copies, preventing monotone accumulation.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Freshness''; claims 21, 22.}
\end{definition}

%% ---------- Huygens escape ----------
\begin{principle}[Huygens escape principle]\label{pr:huygens}
In $d = 3$ spatial dimensions, the wave equation satisfies \emph{sharp
Huygens propagation}: signals propagate exactly on the light cone with no
residual tail.  Consequently, radiated photonic fragments make exactly one
pass through any bounded region and escape into the expanding dark.  This
proves the freshness hypothesis (Definition~\ref{def:freshness}) for the
dominant decoherence channel (photon scattering) without imposing it as an
axiom.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Huygens''; claim 23.}
\end{principle}

%% ---------- Psychological time ----------
\begin{definition}[Psychological time as surprise-weighted record growth]\label{def:psych}
Let $\{e_1, e_2, \ldots\}$ be a sequence of recorded neural events (spikes,
or more generally cognitive state transitions) and let
$h(e_n \mid e_1, \ldots, e_{n-1})$ be the surprisal (negative log predictive
probability) of event $e_n$ given the prior record.  The \emph{internal clock
rate} at record-time $n$ is
\[
  \tau(n) \;=\; h(e_n \mid e_1, \ldots, e_{n-1}),
\]
and the \emph{felt duration} of an interval $[n_1, n_2]$ is
\[
  \Delta T \;=\; \sum_{n=n_1}^{n_2} h(e_n \mid e_1, \ldots, e_{n-1}),
\]
i.e., the growth of the compressed description of what has happened.
A metronome, once learned, contributes $h \approx 0$ per tick.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Psychological time''; claims 27, 28.}
\end{definition}

\section{Main claims and theorems}

%% ---------- Second law ----------
\begin{theorem}[Second law as on-average arrow + record-level museum]
  \label{thm:secondlaw}
The second law of thermodynamics decomposes into two simultaneous movements:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Distribution-level mixing (on-average arrow).}  The
    graphtropy of a coarse-grained macrostate grows on average: distinctions
    between differently prepared systems die away (distinction graph thins).
    Fluctuation theorems (Jarzynski--Crooks family) show this is an on-average
    arrow with permitted reversals on individual trajectories:
    \[
      \bigl\langle e^{-\sigma} \bigr\rangle = 1
      \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
      \langle \sigma \rangle \geq 0.
    \]
  \item \textbf{Record-level museum (strict arrow).}  The same processes write
    durable distinctions elsewhere — broken shells, scorch marks, worn grooves,
    geological strata — constituting a monotonically growing record.
    The world is largely a museum of past thermodynamic events.
\end{enumerate}
Entropy itself is the fluctuating quantity being recorded; the museum of
distinctions is the arrow.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Second law''; claims 9, 10.}
\end{theorem}

%% ---------- Landauer ledger ----------
\begin{theorem}[Landauer ledger]\label{thm:landauer}
Record-writing is a thermodynamic process.  Let $\mathbf{R}(t)$ be a record
maintained by register $\cR$.  The entropy cost of maintaining and erasing
records satisfies an exact accounting:
\[
  \sigma_{\mathrm{total}} \;=\; \sigma_{\mathrm{process}} + \sigma_{\mathrm{record}} + \sigma_{\mathrm{erasure}},
\]
where Landauer's bound $\sigma_{\mathrm{erasure}} \geq k_B T \ln 2$ per bit
erased is one line of this longer ledger.  The reason evidence of past
disequilibrium exists is that record-writing is paid for in the very currency
it documents.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Landauer''; claim 11.}
\end{theorem}

%% ---------- Radiative arrow ----------
\begin{theorem}[Radiative arrow from Huygens escape]\label{thm:radiative}
In a time-varying medium satisfying the following five conditions (none
mentioning relativity):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the medium is driven (optical properties modulated),
  \item the modulation does work on the field,
  \item mode pairs live on a curved unbounded surface,
  \item the drive pushes mode pairs outward into fresh territory,
  \item created excitations decohere,
\end{enumerate}
the system exhibits:
\begin{itemize}
  \item exponential-rate growth (with Anderson-localization statistics),
  \item correlated photon-pair creation from the vacuum,
  \item a strict arrow: the accumulating set of excitation thresholds crossed,
    inscribed in the light.
\end{itemize}
However, an idealized lossless medium is provably time-symmetric; genuine
arrows in real media are inherited from dissipation.  The same skeleton
governs cosmological particle creation during inflation and the dynamical
Casimir effect.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Radiative arrow''; claims 14--19.}
\end{theorem}

%% ---------- Quantum arrow ----------
\begin{theorem}[Quantum arrow from freshness + decoherence]\label{thm:quantum}
Let $\cS$ be a quantum system interacting with an environment $\cE$
satisfying the freshness hypothesis (Definition~\ref{def:freshness}).  Then:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The quantum record — the set of measurement outcomes (distinctions)
    redundantly imprinted in environmental fragments (quantum Darwinism) —
    grows monotonically.
  \item In $d = 3$ spatial dimensions, the freshness hypothesis is proved
    (not assumed) for photonic fragments via the Huygens escape principle
    (Principle~\ref{pr:huygens}).
  \item The quantum arrow reduces to: a measurement outcome is a distinction;
    decoherence is the act of recording it; redundancy is many copies of one
    recorded distinction; the quantum arrow is the growth of that record.
\end{enumerate}
The construction is interpretation-neutral: Everettians read branches,
pragmatists read laboratory outcomes; the mathematics is indifferent.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Quantum arrow''; claims 20--25.}
\end{theorem}

%% ---------- Radiative-quantum unification ----------
\begin{corollary}[Radiative and quantum arrows are two faces of one escape]
  \label{cor:unification}
The forward-onlyness of radiation (retarded waves, Huygens propagation) and
the once-and-forever character of quantum measurement (decoherence via
fresh escaping fragments) are two aspects of the same mechanism: things
leaving and never coming back.  The radiative arrow provides the escape
channel; the quantum arrow is the record written via that channel.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Unification''; claim 24.}
\end{corollary}

%% ---------- Arrow inheritance ----------
\begin{theorem}[Inheritance of arrows down chains of influence]\label{thm:inherit}
Let $\cS_0$ be a far-from-equilibrium source (pump) driving subsystems
$\cS_1, \cS_2, \ldots, \cS_n$ in a chain of influence.  The entropy
production $\sigma_k$ of subsystem $\cS_k$ satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Orientation preservation:}
    $\operatorname{sign}(\langle \sigma_k \rangle) =
     \operatorname{sign}(\langle \sigma_0 \rangle)$ for all $k$.
  \item \textbf{Strength attenuation (data-processing tax):}
    $|\langle \sigma_{k+1} \rangle| \leq |\langle \sigma_k \rangle|$.
\end{enumerate}
Therefore the agreement of the thermodynamic, radiative, quantum, and
psychological arrows in our vicinity is common descent from the same pump
(ultimately gravitational clustering), not coincidence.
The hypothesis list for the inheritance theorem includes the freshness
conditions; for light, these can be derived from escape
(Principle~\ref{pr:huygens}) rather than assumed.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Alignment''; claims 35, 36.}
\end{theorem}

%% ---------- Janus point ----------
\begin{theorem}[Janus-point origin]\label{thm:janus}
For a Newtonian $N$-body gravitating system that never collapses, the
moment of inertia $I(t)$ satisfies the Lagrange--Jacobi identity
\[
  \ddot{I} \;=\; 4K + 2V \;=\; 2E - 2V + 4K,
\]
where $E > 0$ is the total energy.  This forces $I(t)$ to be a convex
function of $t$ with a unique minimum — the \emph{Janus point}
(Barbour--Koslowski--Mercati).  On each side:
\begin{itemize}
  \item The system expands, clusters, and complexifies.
  \item Clustering inscribes durable distinctions (galaxies, stars, strata).
  \item Observers call ``the future'' the direction away from the minimum.
\end{itemize}
The low-entropy ``beginning'' is the waist of an hourglass rather than an
inexplicable initial edge.  The one residual assumption is a mild condition
on correlations at the Janus waist (the ``paper starts unwritten'').
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Origins''; claims 33, 34.}
\end{theorem}

%% ---------- Causal fork ----------
\begin{theorem}[Causal fork and openness of the future]\label{thm:fork}
Under explicit hypotheses about how registers couple to the world, a record
$\cR$ can carry information about its causal past and cannot carry
information about its causal future (Reichenbach's causal fork structure).
\[
  \MI(\cR_t;\; \cS_{\mathrm{past}}) > 0, \qquad
  \MI(\cR_t;\; \cS_{\mathrm{future}}) = 0.
\]
The future ``feels open'' because nothing in the observer's record is, or
could be, a memory of it.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Psychological time''; claim 32.}
\end{theorem}

%% ---------- Psychological time properties ----------
\begin{claim}[Properties of psychological time]\label{cl:psychprops}
Under the surprise-weighted record growth model
(Definition~\ref{def:psych}):
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A metronome, once learned, generates $h \approx 0$ per tick:
    no felt time (boredom).
  \item A stream of surprises writes fast: the interval feels long in
    retrospect (storage-size effect).
  \item The oddball effect (unexpected images seem longer) is near-definitional.
  \item Accelerating years of middle age = declining marginal surprise of a
    life whose statistics have been mostly learned.
  \item In spiking networks, the internal clock rate shows a rise-and-fall
    from order toward deep chaos; hypersynchronized (seizure-like) regimes
    register as clock-stoppage.
  \item The specious present = integration window over which events are bound
    into single recorded tokens.
\end{enumerate}
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Psychological time''; claims 26--31.}
\end{claim}

\section{Cosmological record structure}

\begin{definition}[Cosmological master copy]\label{def:cosmo}
The master copy of the universe's record is the outgoing radiation field,
receding at $c$.  Unerasability and unreadability are the same fact: the
record cannot be destroyed because it cannot be revisited.  Memories, strata,
and archives are local transcripts — partial shadows of a library that
burns outward at $c$.  The CMB is the closest readable page of the master
copy.

The ``blank page'' condition: the clustering condition at the Janus waist
requires the medium start unwritten (uncorrelated), since recording requires
an uncorrelated medium.  Writing proceeds outward from the waist in both
temporal directions.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``Cosmological record''; claim 38.}
\end{definition}

\section{Application to AI: Hyperon AtomSpace}

\begin{definition}[AtomSpace as native causal web]\label{def:atomspace}
In the Hyperon architecture, every inference step creates knowledge atoms
with derivation history attached (premises, rule, timestamp).  The causal
web of events — which the theory says a mind's time is made of — exists
natively as a queryable data structure rather than an inference from timing
and anatomy.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``AI''; claim 40.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Append-only trail and strict arrow]\label{def:appendonly}
A rewritable knowledge store (mutable AtomSpace) carries only an on-average
arrow (beliefs get revised, atoms forgotten).  A \emph{strict arrow} requires
an append-only trail: events written once and never unwritten.  The digital
analogue of the escaping photon is \emph{consensus finality on a distributed
ledger}.  A mind anchored in such a trail has a cryptographically enforced
arrow of time; a mind in mutable memory has an arrow on average and a
promise.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``AI''; claim 41.}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Surprise-weighted knowledge flux]\label{def:flux}
The internal clock of a Hyperon system is the surprise-weighted flux of new
knowledge:
\[
  \tau_{\mathrm{AI}}(n) \;=\; h\!\bigl(a_n \mid \mathbf{A}_{<n}\bigr),
\]
where $a_n$ is the $n$-th new atom and $\mathbf{A}_{<n}$ is the prior
knowledge store.  An AI burning compute in a hot loop re-deriving known
conclusions generates vast logs but $h \approx 0$: no lived time.  A
modest system encountering genuine novelty lives fast.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``AI''; claim 42.}
\end{definition}

\begin{conjecture}[Curiosity as clock maintenance]\label{conj:curiosity}
As a world-model matures, routine input compresses toward zero surprise and
the inner clock decays toward stasis (subjective heat death).  Curiosity —
the drive toward what resists compression (Schmidhuber) — is clock
maintenance: the mechanism by which a maturing mind keeps having a present.
Recursive self-improvement is not just capability growth; it is how a mind
keeps time flowing.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``AI''; claims 43, 44.}
\end{conjecture}

\begin{claim}[Self-modification and identity]\label{cl:selfmod}
Self-modification that preserves the append-only record is coherent
self-transcendence.  Self-modification that rewrites the record is,
strictly, the end of one mind's time and the start of another's.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``AI''; claim 45.}
\end{claim}

\begin{claim}[Shared record as shared time]\label{cl:shared}
In multi-agent systems, a shared append-only record is shared time.
Sub-agents on private mutable stores are opposite-arrow domains waiting to
disagree.  Merging inconsistent histories is an annihilation zone where one
history gets paid down.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``AI''; claim 46.}
\end{claim}

\begin{claim}[Engineering surface]\label{cl:engineering}
In Hyperon, three things invert relative to wet neurons:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The exact record exists natively; coarser views are design choices
    about what to forget — subjective time becomes an engineering parameter.
  \item Memory is the workspace (not a lossy side-channel), but rewritability
    prevents a strict arrow without append-only anchoring.
  \item Surprise valuation is native currency (the system computes it about
    itself), not a theorist's imputation.
\end{enumerate}
The reader that makes the system's history a record for itself is a designed
component; the width of the specious present is a parameter; the structural
correlates of temporal experience are an engineering surface.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``AI''; claim 47.}
\end{claim}

\section{Connections to existing Hyperseed concepts}

\begin{remark}[Graphtropy]
Graphtropy (Definition~\ref{def:graphtropy}) was originally developed for
phenomenology and quantum biology
(\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.00741}{arXiv:1902.00741}).  Its appearance
as the foundation for time's arrow is a unification within the Hyperseed
framework: the same observer-relative entropy measure that structures
conscious experience also structures temporal directionality.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Weakness ordering and the canonical quantale]
The three growth strengths (Definition~\ref{def:growth}) define a partial
order on arrows by ``weakness'': on-average $\prec$ exponential-rate $\prec$
monotone.  The companion paper ``Arrows of Time as Monotone Weakness'' makes
this ordering precise and constructs a canonical quantale from it.  In
Hyperseed terms, this is a lattice of evidential strengths: the quantale
from Evidence/Logic/Energy appears as the algebraic structure governing how
arrows compose, inherit, and degrade.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Observer-relativity]
Observer-relativity is not incidental but foundational: the distinction graph
$\cG_\cO$ makes the observer's resolution the primitive from which entropy,
arrows, and records are derived.  This connects to the Hyperseed principle
that all formal objects are relative to a cognitive frame — there is no
``view from nowhere.''  Recordhood is reader-relative
(Definition~\ref{def:cosmo}), continuous with frame-relativity, as velocity
is relative to a platform.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Quantale structure: Evidence / Logic / Energy]
The signed entropy production $\sigma$ (Definition~\ref{def:ep}) serves
simultaneously as:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Evidence:} $\sigma = \log(P_{\mathrm{fwd}}/P_{\mathrm{rev}})$
    is a log-likelihood ratio, the fundamental quantity of statistical evidence.
  \item \textbf{Logic:} The inheritance theorem (Theorem~\ref{thm:inherit})
    shows $\sigma$ composes under causal chains via data-processing
    inequalities — a logical structure on the flow of distinguishability.
  \item \textbf{Energy:} The Landauer ledger (Theorem~\ref{thm:landauer})
    shows $\sigma$ is paid for in thermodynamic currency — $k_B T$ per bit
    erased.
\end{itemize}
These three faces of $\sigma$ instantiate the Hyperseed
Evidence/Logic/Energy quantale in a concrete physical setting.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Self-similarity of distinction structure]
The framework is self-similar: distinctions are recorded in distinctions; the
medium and the message are the same kind of thing at every level
(Definition~\ref{def:record}).  This mirrors the Hyperseed principle that
metagraphs are self-referential structures where types, instances, and
relations live in the same substrate.
\end{remark}

\section{McTaggart's A- and B-series}

\begin{definition}[B-series as written record; A-series as writing frontier]
  \label{def:mctaggart}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The \textbf{B-series} (tenseless web of events ordered by
    earlier-than) is the written record — the causal web, tenseless because
    the writing is tenseless.
  \item The \textbf{A-series} (tensed past-now-future perspective) is the
    writing frontier — the pen-tip, the locus where the record is currently
    being extended, self-indexed like ``here'' on a map.
  \item The \textbf{feeling of passage} is what it is like to be a record in
    the process of being written; no ghostly flowing substance is needed.
  \item The \textbf{felt present} is distinction-making in progress; the
    \textbf{remembered past} is the record read back.
\end{itemize}
Passage is neither illusion nor metaphysical primitive; it is the
phenomenology of recording and inherits the recording's arrow.
\emph{Provenance: article \S``A- and B-series''; claim 30.}
\end{definition}
