\section{Informal Hyperseed reading}
%% ================================================================

The article's thesis is a single sentence:
\emph{an arrow of time is the growth of recorded distinctions}.
The universe, or some observer-relative piece of it,
keeps records of its own self-differentiation, and
that growth is what ``direction of time'' means.

The argument proceeds by showing that the several classic
arrows of physics---thermodynamic, radiative, quantum,
gravitational, psychological---are instances of one
mathematical structure: a monotonically growing record of
distinctions, indexed by an observer's distinction graph,
whose fluctuating precursor quantities (entropy, field
amplitude, decoherence degree) provide the material being
recorded.

The Hyperseed-relevant content is rich:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Graphtropy} (an existing Hyperseed concept)
      is the foundational formalism: entropy generalized to
      distinction graphs, built from the observer's side.
\item \textbf{Weakness} (quantale-theoretic representation
      of irreversibility strength) classifies the three
      growth regimes: on-average, exponential-rate, monotone.
\item \textbf{Records} are stable correlation patterns---mutual
      information the dynamics protects---and are
      observer-relative in the same controlled sense as
      velocities are frame-relative.
\item \textbf{Genenergy} and \textbf{TransWeave} connect
      through the data-processing inequality that passes
      arrow orientation down chains of influence (arrow
      inheritance).
\item The \textbf{Janus point} origin gives a boundary-condition-free
      account of why all arrows agree, grounded in the convexity
      of gravitational moment of inertia.
\item The \textbf{AI application} (Paper~5) maps the framework
      onto Hyperon's AtomSpace, showing that subjective time,
      curiosity, and self-modification acquire precise
      formal meanings as record-growth phenomena.
\end{itemize}

\section{Core formal objects}
%% ================================================================

\begin{definition}[Distinction graph]
\label{def:distgraph}
A \emph{distinction graph} is a pair $G = (V, E)$ where
$V$ is a set of possible observations (measurement outcomes,
perceptual states, or knowledge atoms) and
$E \subseteq \binom{V}{2}$ is the set of pairs that an
observer, with her actual finite instruments, can tell apart.
Two observations $v_i, v_j \in V$ are \emph{indistinguishable}
at the observer's resolution iff $\{v_i, v_j\} \notin E$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Graphtropy]
\label{def:graphtropy}
Given a distinction graph $G = (V, E)$ equipped with a
probability distribution $p : V \to [0,1]$, the
\emph{graphtropy} $H_G(p)$ is an entropy-like functional
that measures the amount of distinction-making available
at the observer's resolution.  Formally, graphtropy
generalizes Shannon or von Neumann entropy by restricting
the state-counting to the observer's actual discrimination
capacity encoded in~$E$.
When $G$ is the complete graph on $V$,
$H_G$ reduces to the standard Shannon entropy $H(p)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Growth strengths]
\label{def:growthstrengths}
Let $Q(t)$ be a real-valued quantity evolving along a process
indexed by $t$.  Three \emph{growth strengths} are distinguished:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{On-average growth:} $\mathbb{E}[Q(t_2) - Q(t_1)] \geq 0$
      for $t_2 > t_1$, with arbitrarily large downward fluctuations
      permitted on individual trajectories.
\item \textbf{Exponential-rate growth:}
      $\liminf_{t \to \infty} \frac{1}{t} \ln Q(t) > 0$
      almost surely, again with unlimited short-run reversals.
\item \textbf{Monotone growth:} $Q(t_2) \geq Q(t_1)$ for
      all $t_2 > t_1$ along \emph{every} trajectory, never shrinking.
\end{enumerate}
Physical observables essentially never achieve the third.
Only \emph{records}---accumulated histories of thresholds
passed---can be strictly monotone.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Record]
\label{def:record}
A \emph{record} is a stable correlation pattern between a
register subsystem $R$ and a world subsystem $W$: a quantity
of mutual information $I(R ; W)$ that the dynamics protects
against erasure over the timescale of interest.
Records are observer-relative: a pattern is a record with
respect to some decoding dynamics, some actual or possible
interpreter.  A record is \emph{append-only} when new entries
can be added but existing entries cannot be overwritten or
erased without thermodynamic cost (Landauer's bound).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Arrow of time]
\label{def:arrow}
An \emph{arrow of time} relative to observer $\mathcal{O}$
with distinction graph $G$ is the monotone growth of
$\mathcal{O}$'s record of distinctions:
\[
  \mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{O}}(t) \;=\;
  \bigl|\{e \in E_G : e \text{ was first resolved by time } t\}\bigr|
\]
is non-decreasing along every trajectory.  The arrow's
\emph{orientation} is the sign convention inherited from
entropy production; its \emph{strength} is the rate of
record growth.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Entropy production as signed movie-likelihood]
\label{def:entprod}
The \emph{entropy production} of a process $\omega$ over
interval $[0,\tau]$ is
\[
  \Sigma(\omega) \;=\; \ln \frac{P[\omega]}{P[\tilde{\omega}]}
\]
where $P[\omega]$ is the path probability of the forward
trajectory and $P[\tilde{\omega}]$ is the path probability
of the time-reversed trajectory.  The sign of $\Sigma$
is the orientation of the local arrow; its magnitude is
the strength of irreversibility.  This is the fundamental
carrier: treating $\Sigma$ rather than entropy $S$ as
primary is what allows arrows to be inherited down causal
chains.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Thermodynamic arrow: two movements]
\label{def:thermoarrow}
The second law, seen through the record-of-distinctions lens,
decomposes into two simultaneous movements:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Distribution-level mixing (distinction-thinning):}
      distinctions between differently prepared systems die away---cream
      and coffee become mutually indistinguishable at the observer's
      resolution; the distinction graph thins link by link.
\item \textbf{Record-level inscription (distinction-writing):}
      the same mixing processes write durable distinctions
      elsewhere---broken shells, scorch marks, geological
      strata---so that the furniture of the world is a museum
      of past thermodynamic events.
\end{enumerate}
Entropy itself, fluctuating and frame-relative, is the
quantity being recorded.  The museum of distinctions made,
a pattern in the mind of some observer, \emph{is} the arrow.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Freshness hypothesis]
\label{def:freshness}
An environment satisfies the \emph{freshness condition}
when each environmental fragment (photon, air molecule,
thermal mode) arrives at the system of interest
\emph{uncorrelated} with the system's prior state,
interacts \emph{once}, and departs \emph{forever},
never returning to un-tell what it was told.

Formally, let $\{f_k\}_{k=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence of
environmental fragments interacting with system $S$.
Freshness requires:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $I(f_k ; S_{t < t_k}) = 0$ before interaction at time $t_k$,
\item each $f_k$ interacts with $S$ in a single scattering event,
\item $f_k$ is causally disconnected from $S$ for all $t > t_k$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Radiative arrow via driven media]
\label{def:radiativearrow}
In time-varying media (materials whose optical properties are
modulated fast enough that the modulation does work on the light),
correlated pairs of photons are created out of the vacuum.
The state of each mode pair lives on a curved unbounded surface,
and the drive pushes it endlessly outward into fresh territory.
The created photon pairs, once they decohere, are the
\emph{written history}: the canonical arrow is the accumulating
set of excitation thresholds crossed, inscribed in the light
itself.  This structure is shared by cosmological particle
creation during inflation and the dynamical Casimir effect,
unified under five precise conditions (none mentioning relativity).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Quantum arrow as one-pass record-writing]
\label{def:quantumarrow}
A quantum system broadcasts information about itself into
its environment (decoherence).  Once copies are loose,
alternatives can no longer interfere and the outcome is,
for practical purposes, a fact.  The quantum arrow is the
monotone growth of this decoherence record.

Crucially, the monotone character requires the freshness
hypothesis (Definition~\ref{def:freshness}).
For photons in three spatial dimensions, freshness is
\emph{provable} from Huygens' principle: light propagates
sharply on its cone, so radiated fragments make exactly
one pass and are gone into the expanding dark.
Thus the radiative and quantum arrows are welded together
at the level of mechanism: things leaving and never coming back.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Psychological arrow]
\label{def:psycharrow}
The \emph{psychological arrow} is the surprise-weighted growth
of an observer's record of distinctions.  Brain states, as
states, recur (firing rates cycle, sleep resembles sleep),
so no arrow lives at the level of neural state.
But spikes are events; each happens once; the growing web
of spikes-causing-spikes is an accumulating causal record.

Each recorded event is valued by its \emph{surprise}---how
unpredictable it was given everything recorded so far---making
the growing record a compressed description of what has happened.
Felt duration is description length:
\begin{itemize}
\item A metronome, once learned, adds nothing $\Rightarrow$ no felt time (boredom).
\item A stream of surprises writes furiously $\Rightarrow$ long felt duration (storage-size effect).
\item The oddball effect (unexpected image seems longer) becomes nearly definitional.
\item Accelerating years of middle age $=$ declining marginal surprise of a learned life.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[B-series and A-series as record structure]
\label{def:mctag}
McTaggart's two descriptions of time receive precise identities:
\begin{itemize}
\item The \textbf{B-series} (tenseless web of events ordered
      by earlier-than) is the \emph{written record}---the
      causal web of events, tenseless because writing is tenseless.
\item The \textbf{A-series} (tensed perspective of
      past--now--future) is the \emph{writing frontier}---the
      pen-tip, the locus where the record is currently being
      extended, self-indexed the way ``here'' is self-indexed
      on a map.
\end{itemize}
The feeling of passage is what it is like to be a record in
the process of being written.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Janus point]
\label{def:janus}
The \emph{Janus point} is the unique minimum of a convex
function (the gravitational moment of inertia or a suitable
complexity measure) along the dynamical trajectory of a
gravitating system.  From this minimum, the function grows
in both temporal directions, giving rise to a \emph{two-sided}
arrow of time from geometry alone, without requiring a
special low-entropy boundary condition to be imposed by hand.
The one residual assumption is a mild correlation condition
at the Janus waist: the medium starts ``unwritten''
(uncorrelated), since records can only be inscribed on an
uncorrelated substrate.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Arrow inheritance via data-processing inequality]
\label{def:inheritance}
When a far-from-equilibrium source (star, laser pump,
gravitational clustering) drives many subsystems, each
subsystem's asymmetry is a shadow of the source's.
The signed entropy production $\Sigma$ is passed down the
chain of influence:
\[
  |\Sigma_{\text{child}}| \;\leq\; |\Sigma_{\text{parent}}|
  \qquad\text{(data-processing inequality),}
\]
with orientation preserved and strength reduced at each
generation as a data-processing tax.
All observed arrows agree because they share common
descent from the same gravitational pump, whose own
two-sided orientation comes from the Janus point geometry.
\end{definition}

\section{Claims as formal contrasts}
%% ================================================================

\begin{claim}[Core thesis: arrow = record growth]
\label{cl:core}
An arrow of time, wherever it appears in strict (monotone)
form, is the growth of a record of distinctions.  Fluctuating
quantities (entropy, field amplitude) are the things being
recorded; the record itself, accumulated in some observer's
distinction graph, is the arrow.
\end{claim}

\begin{claim}[Loschmidt resolution]
\label{cl:loschmidt}
The Loschmidt stalemate---if entropy increase follows from
reversible mechanics, running any entropy-increasing history
backwards gives an equally legal entropy-decreasing one---is
resolved by recognizing that entropy increase is only an
on-average arrow.  The strict arrow is the record written by
the entropy-producing process, whose monotonicity is a
property of the recording apparatus (append-only register),
not of the fluctuating observable.
\end{claim}

\begin{claim}[Observer-relativity without subjectivism]
\label{cl:obsrel}
Arrows are relative to a way of looking, in a controlled and
lawful fashion, the way velocities are relative to a reference
frame without anyone concluding that motion is an illusion.
The distinction graph captures the precise character of the
observer's coarseness, more cleanly than a partition into cells.
\end{claim}

\begin{claim}[Radiative--quantum welding]
\label{cl:weld}
The forward-onlyness of radiation (retarded waves only) and
the once-and-forever character of quantum measurement
(decoherence) are two faces of the same escape mechanism:
things leaving and never coming back.  This welds the
radiative and quantum arrows together at the level of mechanism,
mediated by Huygens' principle in 3D.
\end{claim}

\begin{claim}[Time-varying media carry an inherited arrow]
\label{cl:tvm}
Time-varying media carry a genuine arrow, but it is
\emph{inherited} from their dissipation (the underlying
resistive physics), not conjured from wave mechanics alone.
The idealized lossless medium is provably time-symmetric.
The materials making the strongest time-interfaces are precisely
those where the inherited asymmetry is maximal.
\end{claim}

\begin{claim}[Felt duration as description length]
\label{cl:feltduration}
Subjective duration is the growth rate of the compressed
description of events experienced.  This accounts for
boredom (metronome $\to$ zero surprise), the storage-size
effect, the oddball effect, and accelerating years, all as
instances of one mechanism.
\end{claim}

\begin{claim}[Common descent of arrows]
\label{cl:commondescent}
The agreement of the several arrows of time around us is
common descent rather than coincidence.  All are children
of the same gravitational pump; the pump's own two-sided
orientation comes from the Janus point geometry.
The inheritance theorem's hypothesis list includes the
freshness conditions, which for light are derivable from
escape (Huygens' principle) rather than assumed.
\end{claim}

\begin{claim}[Records as self-similar correlation structure]
\label{cl:selfsim}
Distinctions are recorded in distinctions.  The medium and
the message are the same kind of thing at every level.
There is no regress of what-records-the-record: each layer
of inscription is more correlation structure, and the
permanence of a record is a dynamical stability property
needing no meta-record.
\end{claim}

\section{Connections to existing Hyperseed ontology}
%% ================================================================

The article's formal apparatus connects to Hyperseed concepts
at multiple levels:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Graphtropy.}
The distinction graph (Definition~\ref{def:distgraph}) and
graphtropy (Definition~\ref{def:graphtropy}) are the article's
starting formalism.  Graphtropy was originally developed for
phenomenology and quantum biology; here it becomes the
foundation for all arrows.  The observer's distinction graph
replaces the standard coarse-graining partition, providing a
richer, more intrinsic representation of observational
resolution.

\item \textbf{Weakness and quantale structure.}
Paper~1 represents growth strengths
(Definition~\ref{def:growthstrengths}) via a quantale---a
complete lattice with an associative tensor product---classifying
arrows by their algebraic strength.  On-average, exponential-rate,
and monotone growth occupy distinct strata in this quantale.
The classification theorem (Claim~\ref{cl:core}) that only
records achieve monotone growth is a statement about the
top stratum.

\item \textbf{Genenergy.}
The surprise-weighted record growth of the psychological
arrow (Definition~\ref{def:psycharrow}) connects to
Hyperseed's genenergy concept: both measure the
information-theoretic work done by a system in creating
novel distinctions.  In the AI application, genenergy
becomes the currency of subjective time---the
surprise-weighted flux of new knowledge atoms.

\item \textbf{TransWeave.}
Arrow inheritance (Definition~\ref{def:inheritance}) via
the data-processing inequality is a TransWeave phenomenon:
signed asymmetry propagating through a web of causal
influence, orientation preserved, strength attenuated.
The common-descent argument (Claim~\ref{cl:commondescent})
is that the TransWeave of arrows has a tree structure rooted
at the gravitational pump.

\item \textbf{Quantale--TransWeave bridge.}
Paper~1's quantale classifies arrow strengths; TransWeave
describes how arrows propagate.  The bridge is the
data-processing inequality: it is both a monotonicity
statement in the quantale (strength can only decrease under
coarsening) and a propagation law in the TransWeave
(children inherit parents' orientation).

\item \textbf{Paper~2: Cartan-pair crystallization.}
Time-varying media produce photon pairs via a mechanism
Paper~2 describes as Cartan-pair crystallization---the
factoring of a symplectic evolution into boost and rotation
components, where the boost (distinction-creating) part
grows monotonically.  This is the radiative arrow's internal
anatomy, connecting to Hyperseed's use of Lie-algebraic
decompositions.

\item \textbf{Paper~3: Neural spiking networks.}
The internal clock rate prediction (inverted-U as a function
of order-to-chaos parameter) was verified in Izhikevich-style
spiking network simulations.  Hypersynchronized (seizure-like)
states register as clock-stoppage, connecting to Hyperseed's
interest in critical-regime computation.

\item \textbf{Paper~5: Hyperon application.}
The AtomSpace derivation history is the AI analogue of the
causal spike web.  Three inversions occur moving from wet
to symbolic substrate:
(a)~resolution becomes a design choice, not a measurement
constraint; (b)~the store is the workspace, but rewritable
memory carries only on-average arrows---strict arrows need
append-only trails (consensus finality / distributed ledger);
(c)~surprise valuation is native, making the internal clock
a computable quantity.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{conjecture}[Graphtropy monotonicity under freshness]
\label{conj:graphmono}
Let $G_t$ be the distinction graph of an observer $\mathcal{O}$
at time $t$, and let the environment satisfy the freshness
condition (Definition~\ref{def:freshness}).  Then the
graphtropy of the joint system--environment state, conditioned
on $\mathcal{O}$'s record, is monotonically non-decreasing:
\[
  H_{G_t}\!\bigl(p_{SE} \mid \text{record}_t\bigr)
  \;\leq\;
  H_{G_{t'}}\!\bigl(p_{SE} \mid \text{record}_{t'}\bigr)
  \quad\text{for all } t' > t.
\]
This would establish that freshness is not only sufficient
for monotone decoherence but for monotone graphtropy growth
in full generality.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Quantale completeness for physical arrows]
\label{conj:quantale}
Every physical arrow of time known (thermodynamic, radiative,
quantum, gravitational, psychological) can be represented as
an element of a single canonical quantale $\mathcal{Q}$
(Paper~1), and conversely, every element of $\mathcal{Q}$
above the on-average threshold corresponds to a physically
realizable arrow.  If true, this would make the quantale
a complete classification of arrow strengths.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Curiosity as clock maintenance in AI]
\label{conj:curiosity}
In a Hyperon-style AI system with surprise-weighted internal
clock (Definition~\ref{def:psycharrow}, AI application),
the subjective time rate $\dot{\mathcal{A}}$ decays toward
zero as the world-model matures (accelerating-years effect)
unless sustained by a curiosity drive that seeks inputs
resisting compression.  Formally:
\[
  \lim_{t \to \infty} \dot{\mathcal{A}}(t) = 0
  \quad\text{in the absence of curiosity,}
\]
and a curiosity-driven policy $\pi^*$ satisfying
$\pi^* = \arg\max_\pi \mathbb{E}_\pi[\dot{\mathcal{A}}]$
maintains $\dot{\mathcal{A}} > \epsilon > 0$ for all $t$.
This would formalize the article's claim that curiosity is
clock maintenance, not motivational garnish.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Self-modification and record coherence]
\label{conj:selfmod}
Self-modification that preserves the append-only record is
coherent self-transcendence (the mind continues its arrow);
self-modification that rewrites the record is, strictly, the
end of one mind's time and the start of another's.
If $\mathcal{R}_t$ is the append-only record at time $t$
and a self-modification $\phi$ is applied:
\[
  \phi(\mathcal{R}_t) \supseteq \mathcal{R}_t
  \;\Rightarrow\; \text{identity continuity}
  \qquad;\qquad
  \phi(\mathcal{R}_t) \not\supseteq \mathcal{R}_t
  \;\Rightarrow\; \text{identity discontinuity.}
\]
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Shared record $\Leftrightarrow$ shared time in multi-agent systems]
\label{conj:sharedtime}
In a hive of cooperating agents, a shared append-only record
constitutes shared time; sub-agents on private mutable stores
are ``little opposite-arrow domains'' whose merger is an
annihilation zone where inconsistent histories meet.
Formally, for agents $a_1, a_2$ with records
$\mathcal{R}_1, \mathcal{R}_2$:
\[
  \mathcal{R}_1 = \mathcal{R}_2
  \;\Rightarrow\;
  \mathcal{A}_{a_1} \parallel \mathcal{A}_{a_2}
  \qquad;\qquad
  \mathcal{R}_1 \neq \mathcal{R}_2
  \;\Rightarrow\;
  \text{reconciliation cost} \geq
  D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\mathcal{R}_1 \| \mathcal{R}_2).
\]
\end{conjecture}

\item Can the five conditions unifying radiative arrows
      (cosmological particle creation, dynamical Casimir effect,
      time-varying media) be derived from a single Hyperseed
      axiom, or do they remain irreducibly plural?

\item What is the precise relationship between the quantale
      of arrow strengths (Paper~1) and the lattice of
      observation frames in Hyperseed's graphtropy formalism?

\item How should Landauer's erasure bound be represented in
      the Hyperseed schema---as a constraint on TransWeave
      edges, a cost functional on record operations, or a
      boundary condition on the quantale?

\item The article's cosmological claim---that the master copy
      of the universe's record is the outgoing radiation field
      receding at $c$---implies unerasability and unreadability
      are the same fact.  Does this have a formal dual in the
      Hyperseed information-theoretic framework?

\item For Hyperon specifically: what is the minimal append-only
      substrate (distributed ledger, Merkle tree, blockchain
      anchor) needed to upgrade AtomSpace's on-average arrow
      to a strict monotone arrow, and what is the computational
      overhead?

\item The inverted-U prediction for internal clock rate as a
      function of order-to-chaos parameter: has this been
      tested in Hyperon's actual attention mechanism, and if
      so, does the seizure-analogue (attentional collapse)
      appear at the predicted parameter values?

\item Can the framework's observer-relativity (arrows relative
      to distinction graphs) be reconciled with the
      perspectival realism of Carlo Rovelli's relational
      quantum mechanics, and if so, does this yield a
      Hyperseed-native account of quantum reference frames?
\end{enumerate}
