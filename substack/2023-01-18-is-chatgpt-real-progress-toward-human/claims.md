### Core claims from the article

1. **Impressive but limited.** ChatGPT demonstrates remarkable fluency and
   broad knowledge but fails on tasks requiring genuine understanding or
   novel reasoning. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Pattern matching vs understanding.** Capabilities arise from
   sophisticated pattern matching, not genuine conceptual understanding.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Missing components.** Key AGI components absent: persistent memory,
   embodied grounding, robust logical reasoning, metacognition, genuine
   creativity. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Scaling limits.** Scaling alone unlikely to produce AGI — qualitative
   capabilities don't emerge from quantitative scaling of current
   architectures. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Hybrid needed.** Real AGI requires hybrid architectures combining
   neural pattern recognition with symbolic reasoning, memory, embodied
   interaction — cognitive synergy. *(source-paraphrase)*

6. **Useful as component.** ChatGPT-like systems are valuable components
   for AGI but are components, not the whole. *(source-paraphrase)*

### Hyperseed-ontology claims

7. **Fiber shortcut.** ChatGPT compresses full fiber traversal (input →
   perception → concept → reasoning → output) into a single neural mapping.
   Fast and often correct but lacks intermediate fiber structure.
   *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

8. **Shortcut fragility.** Within training distribution, shortcut output
   indistinguishable from understanding. Outside, confidently wrong because
   no intermediate fiber catches errors. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

9. **Thin fiber in key dimensions.** Memory fiber ≈ 0, embodiment fiber ≈ 0,
   reasoning fiber thin, metacognition fiber ≈ 0. Impressive breadth,
   insufficient depth. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

10. **Shortcut curvature (d-calculus).** Fiber shortcut has high curvature —
    small input perturbations cause large output changes (hallucinations).
    Full traversal has lower curvature — smooth propagation through
    intermediate stages. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

11. **Missing fiber gradient (d-calculus).** In absent fiber dimensions,
    ∇F is undefined. Qualitative deficiency (dimension absent), not
    quantitative (too small). Scaling can't add missing dimensions.
    *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

12. **Component connection (d-calculus).** ChatGPT contributes a language-
    processing connection (linguistic base → concept fiber). High-quality
    but needs composition with reasoning, memory, embodiment connections
    for full AGI connection. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*

13. **Scaling as volume inflation (d-calculus).** Scaling inflates fiber
    volume without changing topology: vol(F) scales but π₁(F) doesn't.
    AGI requires new topology, not more volume. *(hyperseed-interpretation)*
