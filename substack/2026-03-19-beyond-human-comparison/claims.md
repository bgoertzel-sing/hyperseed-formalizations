   Self-Improvement — substrate-independent dimensions of intelligence.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Orthogonal dimensions.** The four factors are relatively independent.
   *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **Beyond comparison.** True AGI will be so different from human cognition
   that comparison becomes meaningless. *(source-paraphrase)*

5. **Evaluation implications.** Need non-anthropocentric evaluation
   frameworks. *(source-paraphrase)*
