### Core claims from the article

1. **Non-symbolic awareness exists.** Meditative states of awareness
   without symbolic content are real and achievable. *(source-paraphrase)*

2. **Productive paradox.** Symbolic reasoning about non-symbolic states
   is paradoxical but productive, not destructive. *(source-paraphrase)*

3. **Not anti-intellectual.** Non-symbolic awareness coexists with and
   enhances symbolic reasoning. *(source-paraphrase)*

4. **AGI implications.** AGI might benefit from a non-symbolic processing
   mode — not bound by symbolic representations. *(hypothesis)*

5. **Paraconsistent connection.** The symbolic/non-symbolic paradox
   connects to paraconsistency. *(inferred)*
